# Vaultborn

A population sealed inside an object, answering its resonance.

**Why they exist.** They are not descended from anything and were not born. They were
sealed inside an authored object, and they have been answering that object's
resonance ever since. Their qi comes from the resonator they live in, which is
apparently still running, which is the entire reason any of this is knowable.

**The ceiling, and it is a supply problem rather than an anatomy problem.** They clear
the girdling and heel vessels without difficulty — a body sitting in a resonance field
pressurises very evenly — and stall at the seventeenth gate, the first that needs both
flank vessels at once. A vaultborn has **exactly one pressure source**, and one
resonator can press one flank or the other and never both. The vessels are not hard.
There is simply only ever enough for one side.

**Dependence.** Total, and this is the most constrained species in the record. There is
no version of a vaultborn that survives being carried out of the seal. No technique,
no item, no realm changes that, and any account of one that travelled should be read
as a story rather than a report.

**What is not known.** Their anatomy is genuinely under-described. They arrange
themselves along the object's inner surfaces and take their shape from whatever they
are standing on, and nobody who has been inside the seal has returned with consistent
measurements. Whether they reproduce at all is open. What looks like a population may
be one very old thing wearing a lot of faces — which is worth keeping in mind when a
number is reported.

**Why they are in this record.** Because a species that is defined by a container is
the sharpest available demonstration that biology here is downstream of engineering
and law, not the other way round. Everything about a vaultborn is a property of the
object rather than of the vaultborn.
