# Emberhollow

The fire body crossed with the ash body.

**Why it exists.** Nobody chose it. It is what a shared border produces: the volcanic
belt is where the fire-adapted people and the ash-filtering people were already both
living, and the crossing followed the border. It is also high-variance across
generations and everybody involved knows it.

**What it buys.** The fastest cultivation rate in the record, by a margin nobody has
been able to explain, with a qi regeneration rate above both parents and a spiritual
attack output above either. On paper it is the best combat body available.

**What it pays.** Its own rate. The fire half keeps the ash sieve hot enough that it
processes faster and scars faster, and nothing in the record slows it. An emberhollow
is dead of its own cultivation at forty-five. Every account of one contains the word
*fast*, and the word is doing the same work in every sentence.

**The ceiling is the ash parent's, made worse.** Ordinal 8, the liver gate. The ash
parent carried a mineral load in the blood and could not hold the last ordinary
meridian; the fire parent adds a heavier load to blood that is already filtering
stone, so the organ that fails under the burden fails sooner. An emberhollow cannot
hold the liver meridian *and* burns out trying.

**Inheritance is one-directional here, and this is worth recording.** Both parents'
liabilities are inherited and neither parent's advantages compensates:
- mind path: closed by the ash parent, reinforced by the scarring.
- ceiling: 8, structurally the ash parent's, arriving earlier.
- lifespan: shorter than either parent.
- cultivation rate: higher than either parent, and the reason for the other three.

**Where they live.** The belt, and only the belt. The fastest cultivator in the setting
cannot be moved off a rock, which is the boundary between being the best body here and
being a body anyone could rely on.
