# Echoless

Human in every measurable respect, and qi-bearing in none of them.

**Why it exists.** The same mechanism as the Wake, one step further down: a life-form
allowance granted at the floor of the law's range. The world permitted a human body
and did not write the qi component into it. The result is a person.

**What it is.** A Commonborn body, ordinary in every test that works on a Commonborn.
It eats, works, ages and dies on the standard human schedule, at ordinary fertility,
and produces ordinary children who are Echoless children — which is exactly how nobody
noticed for as long as they did.

**What it is not.** Cultivating, in any sense. All three paths are closed, permanently
and without partial exception. It has no qi to store, no refinement to refine, and no
interior to interrupt. An Echoless is not tribulated; it is attended, and then it is
over. It also cannot be compelled by a sect, a debt, a technique or a qi-based poison,
because there is nothing in it for any of those to act on.

**The soul question.** There is no meridian, no dantian, no qi, and no soul that any
instrument in this setting can detect. Whether something is present is not a question
the available methods can answer, and it would be a mistake to report either way.

**Why this is a worldbuilding problem and not a curiosity.** An Echoless can hold any
culture perfectly: language, ritual, manners, memory, all of it, without the least
effort and without the least attachment. Nothing about the body gives anyone a reason
to keep one, so nothing about the body is ever enough to *start* one. This is the
cleanest instance in the record of a species that belongs to no culture for a
mechanical reason rather than a narrative one — and the most reliable test of whether
a given culture is a culture or just a costume. A culture that an Echoless can wear
perfectly and then drop without consequence was never doing anything.

**Do not mistake it for the Wake.** They are opposites from the same cause: the Wake is
entirely made of what other people think was there, the Echoless has nothing at all and
is therefore exactly what everyone expects to see. Every record that confuses them
should be read with that in mind.
