# Carrionfolk

A body that metabolises qi out of the dead and therefore cannot store any.

**Why they exist.** On the ash belt, death is frequent and rich, and a scavenger who
can read qi out of a corpse eats better than a predator who has to win a fight. The
carrionfolk are that scavenger. Given time with a body they can name the realm it
reached and roughly when it stopped, and this is where most of what is known about
the dead of the last four centuries actually comes from.

**The tradeoff that defines them.** Their conduit runs gut to marrow and back with no
dantian anywhere in it. Qi enters in the gut lumen and leaves into the marrow and is
never still. They are the only body in the record with an exceptional *absorption*
rate and a below-baseline *maximum* — they can take in more than anyone and hold less
than a Commonborn.

**The ceiling, and why it is a different kind of ceiling.** Ordinal 11 is not blocked
for a carrionfolk; it is meaningless, and the difference is worth holding onto. The
reservoir gate asks for extraordinary vessels that **hold**. They clear the gate set
and every yin-organ gate on throughput alone and cannot answer a question about
holding, because there is no vessel to hold in. Nothing refuses them. There is simply
nothing there to be refused about.

**What it costs.** A full gut is close to total loss of function, and they starve in
a clean quiet place where nothing dies — which is to say, in a prosperous city. Every
settlement that has ever become rich has driven its carrionfolk out, and every one of
them has come back within a generation, because prosperity produces deaths too.

**Culture, if any.** Graveyards and battlefields end up with the same people near
them, but that is not a culture. It is a gradient. Carrionfolk cluster where death
clusters and they are organised around a queue, not a polity.
