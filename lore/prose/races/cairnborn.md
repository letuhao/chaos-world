# Cairnborn

A body that finished building and stopped.

**Why they exist.** High country builds things that must outlast the builders, and
for a long time nobody could afford masonry that decayed. The cairnborn were the
answer: bodies seeded into a template so that the shape held without maintenance.
They are not an accident of a mineral environment. Somebody cast them, on purpose,
to hold a load.

**The tradeoff.** Mineralising the frame gave them the densest body in the record and
took everything else. Their interior is one cavity with a tidal mineral circulation
that goes everywhere and nowhere. There is no heart to run a channel from and no
kidney to close a loop against.

**The ceiling.** Ordinal 4, the last realm before the yin-organ gate. That gate needs
the heart, small intestine, bladder and kidney as distinct structures with a channel
between each pair. The cairnborn have one cavity. They clear the gate set and then
there is no organ left to run qi through, and they never advance.

**Reproduction.** Cast, not born. Seeding a template takes most of a year, and the
result is fixed from that moment — it does not grow, it does not adapt, and a cracked
plate spreads. This is why a cairnborn's fertility is a count of templates rather
than a count of bodies, and why cairnborn are never *found* in the wild.

**Relation to Stoneborn.** Stoneborn are what you get breeding cairnborn back toward
flesh. The density survives — it is the reason a Stoneborn carries more physique than
anything else here — but the interior comes apart into ordinary organs, the tidal
circulation goes back to a heart, and a dantian becomes thinkable again. Stoneborn
reach further. They are also alive, and they are slower, and they are not the same
thing.
