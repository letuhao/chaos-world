# Stonebound

The documented crossing of the mineral body with the fire body.

**Why it is documented.** Not because the crossing was remarkable. Because the
resulting line was kept and named, which is what a lineage does. The Emberborn line is
recorded alongside the stone line in the game data for exactly this reason: the two
were crossed at one conception and have stayed crossed since, and that is the whole of
the recorded history.

**Inheritance is asymmetric, and always will be.** The mineral parent donates the
ceiling and the durability; the fire parent donates the throughput and nothing else.
A stonebound clears the yin-organ gate no better than a cairnborn does, because a
channel needs two distinct organs and the stone half has one cavity — and the extra
heat does not help, because heat is not a second organ. The mind path is closed and
would stay closed if the frame were twice as dense.

**The lifespan is the interesting part.** Sixty years against the fire parent's
roughly fifty and the mineral parent's three hundred and fifty. The fire half burns
what it takes in immediately and the mineral frame carries the load, so the two together
outlast the fire parent by about a fifth and fall *two thirds short* of the mineral
one. The most durable body anyone can build dies at sixty, and that is not two facts
inconveniently placed side by side — it is one fact. There is no configuration of this
crossing that fixes it.

**Where it lives.** Volcanic high country, because it is the only ground where the two
halves are not fighting each other. Put a stoneborn and a stonebound in the same
climate and the comparison tells you more about both than either record does alone.

**Why the concentration is thin.** One crossing, kept. A bloodline recorded from a
documented event is thin by definition, and `emberborn`'s own bar is set where it is
for that reason.
