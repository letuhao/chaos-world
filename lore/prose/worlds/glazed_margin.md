# The Glazed Margin

## The refusal

Six seats is a budget, not an obligation. This frame took five: physical 2.4, spatial 1.6, qi
3.0, temporal 0.8, life 0.6. Elemental is on the vacant list and one seat sits empty.

It is the only recorded instance in the setting of a tier declining part of its own capacity,
and the refusal has a price that is easier to see than the saving.

**No season runs anywhere in the frame.** Time runs - temporal law is written at 0.8 - but
nothing cycles, because there is no elemental group to make a cycle out of. Fire is
combustion. No elemental can be alive here. And a traveller cannot plan a crossing by
calendar, only by asking somebody who has watched the crossing lately.

## What an unscheduled border costs

Every other spirit frame has a season, so its border has a timetable: open twice a year, shut
on the bad days. This frame's border has none. A pass with no window is not a gate, it is a
line that has to be physically occupied whenever anything might try it - which converts the
border from a piece of scheduling into a piece of standing expenditure.

That is where the Threshold Builders come from. A guild that builds and then guards doors,
walls and thresholds, commissioned to do a job that on every other spirit frame is done by a
calendar. Their whole ethic - a boundary that has never been tested is decoration, and a
builder who has not stood on the far side of their own work has not finished it - is the
ethic of a profession that has been handed the thing a season would otherwise handle.

And the Door Liturgy grows on the same ground: a house marks its own threshold because the
frame beside it will not guarantee welcome, and a mark can be renewed when a law composition
changes.

## The bed beyond it

The crossing attempted here is what failed. A seat cannot be held by somebody who cannot pay
for it every period, upkeep goes from half a unit to as many as five at realm eighteen, and
nobody could. The abandoned crossing left a bed of unmortared seats on the far side, and the
office that sits on it now sells standing to whoever offers most for a place to stand.

So the border is still crossed one person at a time. Not because the crossing is narrow -
because nobody has ever been able to hold it open.
