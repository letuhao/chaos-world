# The Density Ceiling

The oldest unresolved question in the setting that is not about law.

- **The fact.** The densest, longest-lived, most durable bodies stop at realm seventeen. The same
  density that makes them durable is what will not let a cavity form.
- **The offer.** The Full Composition explains it: a six-seat tier with no redundancy is the frame
  in which a fixed body has nothing left to give. Seventeen is where the body has consumed the
  last of it.
- **Why it is still unresolved.** That explanation arrived after the fact. The only surviving
  account is a stonecutter's, and it is an account of symptoms. No one alive was in a position to
  observe the composition being completed, because being in that position required being what the
  dense are not.
- **Why nobody bothers now.** Seventeen is high. The tier above it costs five times as much to
  stand in. A ceiling that nobody below can reach and nobody above needs is cheap to live with.

The unresolved part is not the ceiling. It is that a law with a clean explanation arrived two ages
late, and the setting kept the symptoms and discarded the timing.
