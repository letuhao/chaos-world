# The Full Composition

Realms twelve to eighteen: Spirit Palace through Spirit Ascension.

- **The shape.** Six seats, six laws, nothing doubled. The medium is exactly as large as the
  demand, which is the only condition under which shaping qi is not also spending qi.
- **The consequence for the fixed.** A body that is maximally dense and maximally fixed has no
  cavity left to form at realm seventeen. That is the Density Ceiling, and it is arithmetic.
- **The consequence for the observer.** With zero redundancy there is still nowhere to stand
  outside. The era is complete and therefore closed.
- **Why eighteen is the bridge.** Realm eighteen is the last gate inside this frame, and it is the
  gate that opens onto the first frame with spare seats.

This is the last era in which a cultivator's reach is limited by their own body rather than by
what they can pay.
