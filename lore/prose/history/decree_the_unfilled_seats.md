# The Vacancy Decree

- **What it forbids.** The highest office naming itself into a seat. The office judges no trial it
  caused.
- **Why the prohibition is that specific.** A vacancy in this setting is not an absence of a value.
  It is a seat that exists, is empty, and may be filled by somebody with standing. Writing it as
  zero is what made the earlier attempt legal: you cannot appoint to a zero.
- **Three states, not two.** Does not exist. Exists and its value is vacant. Exists and refused.
  Every screen and every office in the setting uses these three, and they come from here.
- **The cost of the rule.** The office can never fill a seat. It can only name one and hand it to a
  trial it does not sit on, which is why the highest authority in the setting commands nothing that
  can be counted.

This decree is a scar. It exists because somebody once filled a vacant seat by appointment, and
the setting would rather be unable to fill one than repeat that.
