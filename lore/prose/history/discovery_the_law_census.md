# The Law Census

The load-bearing discovery of the whole timeline, and it is a bookkeeping finding.

- **The distinction it established.** `tier_ids` is jurisdiction: where a law may apply.
  `law_slots` is installation: where it actually sits. All six laws apply in all four tiers.
  The mortal tier seats three. The other three apply to it and are not in it.
- **Why this matters more than anything else here.** Every ceiling in the setting follows from the
  seat count, not from any defeat, argument or lineage. Realm nine is a seat count. Realm
  seventeen is a seat count. The unaffordability of realm nineteen is a seat count.
- **What was lost doing it.** The census recorded *which* laws went uninstalled and kept their
  names, which is why anyone can still name a law that no mortal has ever obeyed. It did not
  record *why* three were chosen, and that reason was gone before the second census.
- **The method is the durable part.** Not the answer: the procedure of diffing what a tier
  permits before and after a seat is added. That procedure is what the Sixth Seat repeats.

A reader who knows only one thing about this world's past should know this one: the tiers are
budgets, and the budget was spent.
