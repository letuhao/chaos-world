# The Hundredfold Body

Realms five to nine: Spirit Transformation, Void Refinement, Body Integration, Great Ascension,
Tribulation Crossing.

- **The size cap.** A mortal world tops out at a hundred. There is no seated spatial law to grow
  it. The number is a ceiling on the frame, not a measurement of the frame.
- **The life-form list.** Plants and beasts. No humanoid, no elemental. That is the intelligence
  ceiling stated as a roster: a mortal tier will not admit forms that could exceed its own seat
  budget.
- **What the era produced.** Great Ascension and Tribulation Crossing, the last two mortal gates,
  both closed by the frame rather than by anyone.
- **Why it is named for the body.** These five realms are all about making something hold its
  shape, in a tier that is the only one whose shape can be guaranteed.

This is the era in which the setting stopped being a place where people fail upward and became a
place with a documented top.
