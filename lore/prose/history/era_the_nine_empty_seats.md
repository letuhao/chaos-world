# The Nine Empty Seats

Named for the tier above; this era is the immortal one, and the name is the debt the top tier left
its shoulders.

- **The arithmetic.** Ten seats, six laws, four vacant. The immortal tier is the first frame with
  genuine slack.
- **Why slack is a political problem and not a gift.** An office with a vacant seat is an office
  somebody could fill. The first time somebody filled one by appointment, it did not go well. See
  the Vacancy Decree.
- **What the era built.** The one form of authority that survives an unfilled seat: an office that
  names a seat and may not take one. Authority without occupancy, which is the only kind this era
  could afford.
- **Why nine and not four.** The transcendent tier's surplus belongs to the Still Point. This era
  is only the part of the debt that was already owed before anyone climbed into it.

Four vacant seats in a tier of nine realms is the whole reason the setting's politics is written
in the vocabulary of vacancy.
