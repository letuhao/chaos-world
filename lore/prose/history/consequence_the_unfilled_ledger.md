# The Unfilled Ledger

The last thing in this timeline and the only one still being decided.

- **The position.** Nine vacant seats in the transcendent tier, four in the immortal, and no
  procedure in any ledger anywhere that can certify any of them empty.
- **Why the top tier is not crowded.** Upkeep at the top is the most expensive in the setting. The
  only thing that pays for it is the unclaimed capacity itself. Abandoning the tier loses the
  revenue and keeps the bill, so it is cheaper to stand in it than to leave it.
- **Why the lower tier is not given up.** One faction holds regions in both the immortal and the
  transcendent tiers. The lower one is the route, the supply and the legitimacy of the upper one.
  Giving it away would mean paying to stand in the top tier out of pocket, and nobody can afford
  that.
- **The open question.** Whether any of the nine can be certified empty. If one can, the setting
  has a seventh law to find and a procedure for refusing it. If none can, the top tier is
  permanently a measurement that cannot be completed, and the observer stays.

The three states of a vacant seat, the rule that no office may take its own, and the four vacant
immortal seats all trace back to this one unanswerable question.
