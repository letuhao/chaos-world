# The Ashfall Belt

The volcanic arc whose ash-fall fans are the only ground in the setting where the air is a
delivery mechanism for rock.

Elemental law is unspent across the lower bands, so the arc's element is *starving*, not merely
hot. It emits. It does not work. Nothing on the belt burns the way fire burns on the licensed
ground above the stair - the same technique that fails at full strength everywhere else fails here
too, and what the belt actually delivers is a continuous fall of grit, sulfur-root and
unprocessed mineral with no season attached to it.

The consequences are all downstream of that one fact.

The lichen that skins the fans is the only thing here that fixes stone into something a body can
eat, so the lichen is the base of the belt's entire food chain. It is also the belt's only
record: it records fall thickness in its own growth, so a quiet decade is legible on the rock
years after the arc stopped being anyone's business.

A body built to breathe this air gives up smell entirely. A chambered nasal sieve lined with cilia
traps the grit and passes it into a crop for grinding, which is why the belt's people taste
minerals the way anyone else tastes salt and are the only population that can tell two ash grades
apart by tongue. Their blood carries a suspended mineral load that filters through the liver, and
the liver is the first organ to fail under the burden - the eighth gate completes the ordinary
meridian set there, so the ceiling and the diet are the same fact.

A whiff of a soft poison is a wound to such a body. There is no second line, because a second
line would mean a second filter, and the first one already has more than it can do.

Nothing here is quiet, and nothing here is safe. The scavengers are the only population that
*thrives*, and they thrive on the aftermath rather than on the killing, which is why the belt has
never once produced a predator worth the name.
