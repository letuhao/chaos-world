# Jade Cleft

The far side of the Sixfold Ridge, where the jade seam runs in a narrow cold cleft no road reaches except through the Gale Pass.

It exists because jade holds qi, so the cleft is simultaneously the spirit tier's best refinement ground and its worst farming ground - a place nobody can live on that everybody worth cultivating wants to be able to reach.

That combination is what makes it the ridge's real prize and its real dispute: a resource you cannot settle, settle nowhere near, and can only get to through one door that somebody else holds.

The cleft is where the spirit tier's practitioners actually come for, and it is the worst place on the ridge to live. That is not an accident of the map; it is the shape of the resource. Jade holds qi because nothing about it wants to give it up, so the deposit that makes the cleft valuable is the same property that makes the ground on top of it barren.

Which means every claim on the cleft is made from somewhere else. Nobody who controls the cleft controls the pass, nobody who controls the pass has to enter the cleft, and the two arrangements have coexisted badly for as long as both deposits have been worth mining.
