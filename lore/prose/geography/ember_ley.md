# Ember Ley

The first ground in the setting where fire is simply fire: an elemental seam in the Immortal Court, licensed because this tier writes elemental law and writes it twice.

It exists because the mortal and spirit tiers left elemental_law unspent, so an Emberblood affinity and every elemental technique worked at full strength and produced nothing - here the same technique works, and that asymmetry is the whole reason the ley matters.

What makes it worth holding is that everyone who has ever been refused an effect below this tier came here to be refused no longer, which makes it the setting's most wanted valley and the least holdable one.

Elemental law governs dominant elements, elemental balance and seasonal cycles. It is unspent in the lowest two tiers, and an unspent law does not weaken what tries to use it - it removes it entirely. A fire technique on the plains runs at full strength and cannot act at all.

The ley is where that stops being true, and the asymmetry is enormous: the same practitioner, unchanged, is a different asset. This is the strongest economic fact in the setting and it is not a market or a doctrine, it is a valley. Every fire-affinity body that can travel is drawn to it, and nothing about the ley's geology resists being drawn to.
