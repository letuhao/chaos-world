# Cairn Field

The ring of trial grounds holding every mortal-band breakthrough from realms one to nine, on the one tier that licenses no humanoid to attempt one.

It exists because those nine trials are the only thing on the Mortal Plains anybody would walk a long way to watch, so a plain with no practitioners and no export is sustained entirely by spectators - and the ground is held, taxed and raided for the gate fees.

What makes it worth holding is that the gate fees on that spectacle are the only tax the plains can actually collect, because a crowd can be counted twice and ore cannot.

A mortal-tier world holds three imprints and licenses plant and beast life, so every humanoid on it is a visitor. That single fact decides the Cairn Field: the tier's most valuable ground is not where its own people live, because its own people are not licensed here, but where its people come to be watched.

The field is maintained by people who cannot attempt anything on it. The cairns are stacked by households that gain nothing from a breakthrough but everything from the crowd, and the crowd is the only export a resource-poor tier has.
