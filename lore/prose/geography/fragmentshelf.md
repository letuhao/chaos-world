# Fragmentshelf

The shelf where dao_fragment is cut in the Transcendent Realm: ground that is itself a broken piece of a law, workable only while the break is fresh.

It exists because fragments cure as they decay, and a cured fragment is void crystal, so the shelf's two authored resources are two stages of one wound and only the first stage is worth digging.

What makes it worth holding is that it is worthless soon: the shelf is worked hardest by whoever understands the deadline and fought over hardest by whoever does not, which is the oldest argument in the setting and the only one with a schedule attached.

This is the only place in the setting where a resource gets worse while you hold it. Everything else on the shelves of every tier above the plains can be left alone; a dao fragment cannot, because it is busy becoming something else.

Which makes the shelf an argument about time rather than about territory. Two parties can hold different halves and both be right about who should have it, and the tie is broken by who can break a fragment fastest - the only contest in the setting where the winner's prize is smaller than the winner's.
