# Terrace Ring

The immortal-band trial ring, realms nineteen through twenty-seven, standing on the court's upper ground above the Ember Ley.

It exists because this tier runs at five to fifty times a slower world's clock, so a failed breakthrough injures the ground as well as the body, and the ring is the only place in the setting where the scars of past attempts are still legible on the rock.

What makes it worth holding is that those scars cannot be edited by whoever lost them, which makes this the only arena in the setting whose record is worth trusting.

Fast time makes failure archaeological. A mortal-band failure leaves a body; an immortal-band failure at this tier's clock leaves a mark that is still readable a season later, which makes the ring the setting's only court record that cannot be edited.

The ring also sits above a licensed elemental seam, so the pressure the trials apply is applied to ground that is already holding more law than the practitioner is. It is the only arena where the floor is the harder problem.
