# The Saltpan Basin

A closed evaporite basin on the Mortal Plains' dry margin, holding a standing brine whose level
is the basin's only boundary and whose saturated floor will hold a body that nothing else will.

It matters for one reason and the reason is arithmetic. A mortal world spends three of its six
law slots on physical, life and qi, which leaves spatial law unwritten. The basin therefore
cannot drain, cannot move and cannot be surveyed out of. There is no mechanism here that changes
its shape, so nothing about it is a matter of opinion.

That is what makes the brine line a border. No treaty raised it and no treaty can lower it: the
line is wherever the water is, and the water does not read. A dominion whose edge is a species'
metabolism has an edge no two parties ever had to agree on, which is why the quarrel about the
tariff at the mouth of every import road is so much older than the quarrel about the brine.

Three zones, in order of hostility to anything that is not already adapted:

- **The pan surface.** Walkable and saturating. A body that cannot drink fresh water fails here
  in about nine days, and rain is a hazard rather than a blessing.
- **The margin.** Where the fresh inflow and the brine are both within reach, which is the only
  ground in the setting a body needing two chemistries can stand on without travelling between
  them. This is why the cross spread along the trade routes rather than across them: it has no
  homeland, only a shoreline somebody else schedules.
- **The saturated floor.** Where the brine layer is thick enough to support a body in it
  permanently. It is below the line for everything else, and a body taken above it stops within
  hours.

The setting has one collapsed population on this ground and the collapse had no visible cause.
The mat that feeds the basin grew to whatever concentration the water currently held, so when the
chemistry changed the mat followed it perfectly and the bodies did not. Nothing about the ground
looked wrong. That is the finding: the basin's failure mode is invisible from inside it.
