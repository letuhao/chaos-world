# Bitter Scrub

The only ground on the Mortal Plains where herb_common grows, and therefore the tier's only medicine, its only dye and its only calories that keep.

It exists because the scrub is slow, the grazing herds are faster, and a crop nobody can fence around cannot be defended - so the resource the whole tier runs on is also the resource nobody can own.

That is what turns it into a quarrel: the same plant is medicine, feed and famine, so a herder who grazes it in the dry months has consumed the year's supply of a remedy, and the households that buy that remedy every year are the households arguing hardest.

The scrub does not have a bad year and a good one. Elemental law governs seasonal cycles and it is unspent, so nothing here is dormant, nothing is seeded for a spring, and regrowth is a straight function of how hard the ground has just been grazed. The scrub is a ledger, and it is legible from the air.

The consequence is that the famine is not caused by weather. It is caused by arithmetic: a herd's winter feed and a household's year of medicine are the same plants, the herds are larger, and the households are the ones who cannot move. Every dispute over the scrub is really a dispute over who is allowed to be hungry in a bad year.
