# Rift Shore

The one stretch of the Transcendent Realm's edge where a rift has stood open, because the top budget is the only one with enough imprints gathered close enough to push one off the end of its range.

It exists because a rift closes when the value comes back inside its range, so the shore is a door with a timetable rather than a permanent hole - and a door with a timetable can be guarded, taxed and bargained over like any other crossing.

What makes it worth holding is that it is the only road between worlds in the entire setting, and every power that has ever reached it has built its wall facing the wrong way to stop people walking out.

Nothing else in the setting makes two worlds adjacent. Not power, not a technique, not an argument with the right cosmology - a tear only opens where an imprint has been pushed outside the range it was written at, and only the top tier has the density of imprints to push one.

Which means the shore is the only place a migration can happen, and every party that has ever stood on it has correctly calculated that the threat from outside is real and has therefore built facing in. The setting's one door is defended by people who are guarding the wrong side of it.
