# Spiritscar Quarry

The worked face in the Sixfold Ridge where spirit stone is actually cut, and the evidence for the Density Ceiling because it is a hole you can stand in and see the end of.

It exists because a quarry is the only institution that measures a world instead of praising it: every block cut is a measurement of how much the ridge had, and the answer has been falling for three generations.

That makes it the setting's first honest ledger and the reason the density ceiling is believed rather than asserted - a claim backed by a hole can be checked by anyone willing to climb down, which is precisely why whoever benefits from the ceiling does not want the hole open.

A quarry answers the one question a world cannot answer about itself. How much is there? Not how much there was, not how much the ledgers claim, but how much - which means someone has to go and take it out of the ground and count.

The answer has been falling, and the reason that matters is not scarcity. It is that the trend is public. Anyone can verify a density ceiling by digging, and a ceiling that anyone can verify is not a rumour or a doctrine or a guild's convenient excuse - it is a fact about the ground, and the only way to argue with it is to dig differently.
