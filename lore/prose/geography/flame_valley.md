# Flame Valley

Flame Valley is the one valley in the setting where fire does what fire is for: a long cleft on the Spirit Peaks where elemental law is written, so every fire technique that failed at full strength below this tier works here.

It exists because elemental_law is unspent at the mortal and spirit bands' lower edge and written from here up, so the valley is the first ground on the ladder where an Emberblood affinity is an asset rather than a full-strength ability with nothing to act on.

What makes it worth holding is that everyone who has ever been refused an effect on the plains came here to be refused no longer, and a valley everyone converges on is a valley that cannot be held by whoever found it.

The two dragons here are not the reason the valley matters. The reason is arithmetic: an elemental is a life form no law licenses below the immortal band, and an elemental technique produces absolutely nothing where elemental law is unspent - not less, nothing. The valley is the first ground where the technique has something to push against.

It sits behind the Gale Pass, which means every claim on fire in the setting is also a claim on the only road to it. That is the whole political shape of Flame Valley: not who holds the valley, but who holds the calendar on the pass.
