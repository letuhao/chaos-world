# Gale Pass

The single crossing of the Sixfold Ridge, and the reason the ridge is a border instead of a wall.

It exists because the spirit tier writes spatial and temporal law, so a pass can be surveyed, closed and reopened on a schedule - which makes it the setting's first choke point and its first place where holding ground means holding a timetable.

What makes it worth holding is that it is the only supply route to the jade cleft and the only road to Flame Valley, so every dispute about who may cross is a dispute about who may have what they need.

A wall with one door is not a border; it is a toll. The pass is the mechanism that turns the ridge's geography into somebody's revenue, and the reason that revenue is unusually stable is that the schedule is set by the season rather than by the gatekeeper.

The stability is what makes it worth attacking. A holder who can justify the window from the Tidemark can hold the pass for years without a single obvious act of aggression - which is the most durable form of control in the setting, and the one most likely to be disputed by somebody who has read a season table and worked out where the slack is.
