# Fragment Ring

The transcendent-band trial ring, realms twenty-eight through thirty, standing on the Fragmentshelf where the ground is itself a broken law.

It exists because a top-tier breakthrough is arithmetic rather than combat, and this is the only substrate in the setting that can host a self larger than its container without collapsing - so three attempts have ever been possible here, and fewer than that have ever been survived.

What makes it worth holding is that there is no second substrate anywhere in the setting: three trials, one piece of curing ground, and nowhere else to be sent if it fails.

Mind Dao reads spatial law as an exit rather than a container, which means the last three realms require a self that does not fit inside its body. Nothing else authored can hold that; broken law can, because a break is the only kind of ground that is not trying to contain anything.

Three trials, three substrates competing, and a ground that is curing while they are attempted. The ring is the setting's most exclusive piece of ground and the least permanent.
