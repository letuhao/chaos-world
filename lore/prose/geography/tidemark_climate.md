# The Tidemark

The anchored season that decides when the Cloudshelf Sea can be crossed and when the Gale Pass is safe, which this tier can have precisely because elemental law is written here.

It exists because a calendar is the first form of wealth: whoever reads the Tidemark sets the shipping dates, and a shipping date is a law that everyone downstream obeys without knowing who wrote it.

What makes it worth fighting over is that it is legible to anyone with a season table and not obvious to anyone without one, so control of the crossings has been handed to whoever was first willing to be boring about the sky.

The mortal plains have weather and no season. That is not a poverty of observation - it is the elemental slot being unspent, which means nothing in that world holds the year to a rhythm. Everything below the spirit tier is therefore operating in weather, and weather cannot be scheduled, charted or inherited.

Above it, the year becomes an address. The Tidemark says when the shelf is passable and when the pass is survivable, and a published season is a piece of authority that costs nothing to hold and cannot be seized. This is the setting's first soft power and it is not going to stay soft.
