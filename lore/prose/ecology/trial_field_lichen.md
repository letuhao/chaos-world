# Trial Field Lichen

A grey lichen that colonises worked stone wherever a large quantity of qi has been drawn through it in one place, which makes it the only reliable marker that a trial has ever been held on this ground.

It exists because drawn qi leaves a trace a plant can live on, and on the ring stones that trace never washes off - so the Cairn Field, the Sixth Seat Grounds, the Terrace Ring and the Fragment Ring are all legible from above as pale patches.

That makes it a court record nobody can edit: whoever lost an attempt on this ground is still marked out in lichen, and on the Terrace Ring at five to fifty times time the mark outlives the argument about what happened.
