# Storm Elemental

The setting's one authored elemental: a life form licensed only at the immortal and transcendent tiers, reaching combat power 100 to 1000, and impossible anywhere else at any strength.

It exists because elemental law is the group that licenses an elemental, and it is unspent in the two lower tiers - so this is not a creature that is rare below the Ember Ley, it is a creature that has no lawful existence there at all.

That is why the ley is a destination and not a hunting ground: an elemental body below the licensed tiers runs at full strength and produces nothing, which is a worse outcome than being weak, because nothing about the creature reports the problem.
