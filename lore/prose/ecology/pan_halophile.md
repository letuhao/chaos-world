# Pan Halophile Mat

The salt-tolerant mat that skins the basin's brine and crusts the pan surface. It is the
setting's only food base that a brine-bearing body can use at all.

The mat grows to whatever concentration the basin currently holds. That single property is the
whole of its relationship to the water, and it is also the whole of the disaster it eventually
recorded.

A body whose taste reads brine across four orders of dilution is reading the mat as much as the
water - it cannot tell two salts apart, but it can tell concentration to an accuracy nothing else
can match, and the mat is where that accuracy is checked against something. On the pan that check
works. The mat thickens as the brine concentrates and thins as it dilutes, and a body reading the
water correctly reads the mat correctly too, for as long as the two agree.

They stopped agreeing, and the mat did not fail. The chemistry of the brine changed; the mat
tracked the change exactly as it tracks concentration; and every body that needed the old
concentration went on reading the water perfectly well, and finding the new mat edible, and
declining over three generations.

There was no visible cause because there was nothing to see. The ground was doing precisely what
the ground does. The replacement crust was food and it was nutritionally empty, which is a
sentence nobody involved found useful at the time.

The mat is still there. It is doing what it does. It is the only reason a basin with no
agriculture has ever supported a body heavier than a person, and it will go on supporting
whatever still tolerates the water it is currently growing in.
