# Spirit Herb

The only plant authored into all four world tiers at once, and therefore the setting's single most portable resource.

It exists because life_law is one of the three groups a mortal-tier world does write, so the lowest tier can grow a remedy - and the same species is authored in the top tier, which makes it the one good a mortal herbalist and an immortal alchemist can both trade in.

That portability is what makes it the spine of every economy between the tiers: it is the only resource whose supply does not depend on a resource existing, and its absence from any one tier would be a hole in the world's own law budget rather than a shortage in the ground.
