# Cavern Lumen Fungus

The luminous hyphal mat that skins every wet wall of the deep caverns. It emits light. It cannot
receive it.

That is not the interesting fact. The interesting fact is that it reads a hand on a wall by the
pattern of its own response to being touched, which is the only way a lightless population
navigates: not by seeing the wall, but by watching the wall react.

The colony is not an organ a body happens to have. It is threaded through the dermis and into the
spinal cord, the human tissue is the substrate, and neither half can be removed from the other
and survive. The body is what the fungus lives in and the fungus is what the body reads with,
and the seam between them is not a seam.

Its meridian network is a highway rather than a pipeline, because the colony transacts with the
surrounding qi directly instead of storing it. That is why the population has no structural
ceiling of the ordinary kind: nothing it does asks an organ to hold anything.

There is a cost, and the cost is the reason the cavern population is a population and not a
curiosity.

Strains do not mix. A host pair must find a compatible strain within the first year or the
colony rejects the child - not gradually, not partially, but outright. Fertility is low and the
pairing is a rite with a deadline, which means the caverns have always had a scarcity that has
nothing to do with qi.

And a colony outside the caverns dies inside a season. Dry, sunlit or vibrating ground is fatal,
and fire is faster. Which is why the one population that ever got out from under it did so by
accident: a lineage lost its strain and a mineral bloom able to survive the same air colonised
the wound instead. That body sees in the dark. That body also lives a third as long.

The fungus is still down there. It is still doing the only thing it does, which is answering.
