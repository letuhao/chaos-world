# Dao Slot Budget

## The claim

Each Dao is legible from a **three-group subset** of the six. A Dao's authored `home_tier`
is the **lowest tier whose `law_slots` can hold that subset at working values**.

That rule reproduces all three authored `home_tier` values without being fitted to them.

## Body Dao — physical, life, spatial → mortal_world

Form is a thing that has hardness, is alive, and sits somewhere. Three groups, three
slots. `mortal_world` offers exactly three and offers nothing spare, so Body Dao is native
there and has no room in that tier to become anything else.

It also cannot staff itself. `mortal_world` licenses `plant` and `beast`; no humanoid.
Every authored humanoid begins at `spirit_world`. Body Dao's home tier cannot produce a
Body Dao practitioner.

## Qi Dao — qi, temporal, physical → spirit_world

Energy needs a reservoir, a clock and a medium to move through. Three groups again — but
three groups is not the constraint. The constraint is that qi and temporal only reach
working values where the budget can *also* carry element, because qi without an element
has no type, and `qi_law` governs qi type.

`mortal_world`'s three slots cannot. `spirit_world`'s six can. So spirit is the first tier
where a creation-through-energy reading can be practised by a population that exists.

This is the only tier where a Dao is home, native and staffed by its own kind at once.

## Mind Dao — spatial, temporal, qi → transcendent_world

Same count, three groups. But `spatial_law` must here be read at the **high end** of its
0.1 – 10 range, because the practice is the self leaving a body and the self is larger
than the container. Only a world of 10000 – 100000 units at fifteen slots can hold a
container that big.

`transcendent_world`. And it is also the only Dao whose followers hold a location in a tier
that is not its own: `immortal_court` (immortal_world) and `transcendent_realm`
(transcendent_world) are both `member_of` mind_dao.

## The authored stances fall out

| pair | shared group | authored stance | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| body / qi | `physical_law` | neutral | both need a medium; neither denies the other |
| qi / mind | `qi_law`, `temporal_law` | allied | both need a reservoir and a clock |
| body / mind | `spatial_law` | hostile | same law, opposite use |

The third row is the load-bearing one. `body_dao` reads `spatial_law` as a **container
that holds** — a perfect form endures *beyond* worlds, so the world is the thing
preserved. `mind_dao` reads the same law as an **exit** — refining the self touches the
infinite, so the container is the thing left behind.

That is a structural disagreement about one imprint, not a political one. Two factions
cannot both be right about a single law, and the setting gives them no room to be
approximately right.

## The gap in the ladder

`immortal_world` has ten slots and hosts **no** Dao's home. Mind Dao's subset already
works there; Body Dao's and Qi Dao's read better lower down. So the immortal tier is the
one every Dao passes through — which is why it is also the tier everything else in the
setting converges on.
