# Realm Band

## A realm is a reading, not a rank

The 30-realm ladder (ADR 0005) is usually described as a ranking: higher is better. Under
the law composition it is better read as a **band of tolerated pressure**.

A realm number is the range of law values a body can hold without ceasing to be a stable
container. Above the band, the container fails. That is why the four authored tier bands
are contiguous — 1-9, 10-18, 19-27, 28-30 — and non-overlapping, and why each band's
upper edge coincides with the next tier's law budget rather than with any change in the
practitioner.

| tier | band | slots | what the band's edge is |
|---|---|---|---|
| mortal_world | 1 – 9 | 3 | three imprints carry a body to 9 and no further |
| spirit_world | 10 – 18 | 6 | six imprints, plus element, carry it to 18 |
| immortal_world | 19 – 27 | 10 | ten imprints over two layers carry it to 27 |
| transcendent_world | 28 – 30 | 15 | fifteen imprints over three layers, and the ladder ends |

## Not a merit ranking

The same number in two tiers describes two different things.

A `realm_min` 10 in a spirit-tier world is the bottom of a six-slot budget with element
and qi at working values. A spirit-tier **realm 10** is not "better than" a mortal-tier
realm 10 — it is a reading of a different world, against a different set of imprints.

Practically this is why a cultivator moving up a tier is not promoted so much as
**re-based**: the same body, re-read against a stricter set of values.

## What breaks first past the band

The body, never the technique. In order: the meridian network is the first structure to
fail, because it is the most numerous and the least redundant; the sea is the last,
because it is the only reservoir. A technique that worked at the top of a band produces
nothing at the top of the next one — not less, nothing — and the distinction is the same
one a law void makes.

## The tide interaction

A tide moves a law's value inside its authored range. So the top of a realm band is
**touchable** at a crest and not holdable in the trough.

This is the entire reason breakthroughs are scheduled rather than merely attempted: a
practitioner at realm 9 in a mortal world can be at the edge of realm-9 tolerance during
a tide and back inside it immediately after. The band's ceiling is a property of the
world's setting, not of the student's effort — which is what stops "train harder" from
being the universal answer to a ceiling that is arithmetic.

## The band is not negotiable

The ladder stops at 30 because thirty realms are authored. That end of the ladder is a
content decision, not a cosmological one, and nothing in this record should be read as
arguing that a band past 30 could not exist — only that the setting has not authored one.
