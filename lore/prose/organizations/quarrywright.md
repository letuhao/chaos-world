# The Quarrywright

## What it is for

To cut and sell the only material in the mortal tier dense enough to hold a forming core.

Iron ore is the entire resource list below the Spirit Peaks. A core has to be caged in
something. So the third gate of the ladder was a mining problem a long time before it was a
spiritual one, and the guild that worked it out is the reason the tier has a metallurgy at
all.

## What it spends

Bodies it does not have. Nothing native to this frame is licensed to work the face at a realm
that can cut it, so the guild hires practitioners arriving down the stair and pays them at a
rate that has nothing to do with what the tier is worth. Every shift at the face is staffed by
a visitor.

It also spends the flats' claim. There is no legal right to the Ironhead Flats - only the
working face - and the flats are worth holding only while the seam is being worked. Every
resident is a temporary occupier by construction, which means none of them has standing to
complain about terms. The guild knows this and prices it.

## What it keeps

The seam key, and the face rather than a claim. Holding the face holds the deposit; there is
no deed on ground that cannot be measured, and there is no measurement.

## What happens to members who leave

They forfeit the seam key and are marked on the flats as one who left a face unrepaired. On
ground where the only record is a marker standing, that mark is permanent and there is no
authority that can lift it.

## The two guilds on one seam

`organizations.spoilwright` clears the heap - forty years of spoil that cannot disperse,
because with no spatial law there is no process on which it would. Every barrow it removes is a
barrow of shift the Quarrywright does not pay for, and the face cannot advance until the heap is
gone. The resource and the access to it are permanently in opposition, and neither guild can
buy the other.

`organizations.ironpact` is the third party and the reason the terms are bad: a roster that
answers a standing order cannot be moved for an urgent shift, so the guild either buys capacity
at a price somebody else's roster set, or loses the shift and the seam with it.

## One thing it does not report

The Quarrywright knows about `worlds.first_ward_chamber`, the pocket on the flats where
distance is conserved. It has never reported it. A pocket in which a survey agrees with itself
is the only place on the tier where a boundary could be walked, and reporting it would end the
arrangement that makes the work possible.
