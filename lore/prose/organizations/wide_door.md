# The Wide Door

## What it is for

To place a body on the far side of a tear that the far side has agreed to accept.

Nothing in this setting sells houses. Nothing sells a road. This sells a *tolerance*.

## Why tolerance is the product

A world boundary is not a wall between neighbours; it is the edge of a law budget. Crossing it
means arriving where a group you relied on is unwritten. An unwritten law is not a weak law -
it is an absent one, with no dial and nothing to push - so a technique built on it fails at
full strength rather than at reduced strength.

A body is built to the imprint set it grew in. The higher a body is tuned, the more finely it
reads one frame and the more brittle it is in another, so a realm is a passport and a fragility
at once. What can be sold across a boundary is therefore not power. It is a body the other side
will not reject.

Power opens nothing. That is the whole finding, and it is why this is a network and not a
sect.

## What it spends

Bodies held on the near side as cover - and the near side is where they can be seen. The lodge's
capital is other people's visibility, and it borrows it rather than earning it.

## What it keeps

Nothing. There is no roster and no membership. A name used at the far side stays used.

## What happens to members who leave

Nothing happens, and that is the finding rather than a gap in the record. A person used at the
far side cannot be dismissed from a thing they were never shown was there, and a body that has
been sold across a tear is not a body anybody can recall to this side of it.

Leaving is not an available operation. This is the only institution in the setting where that
is true, and it is worth noticing that nobody describes it as a hardship.

## Why nobody can investigate it

It keeps no record, publishes no rate and has no address. The only evidence of it is a name
that appears at a place where names are read.

That is exactly the evidence the lodge is built not to leave, and it is why three bodies that
depend on names existing - the counting house that publishes credit, the writ that keeps a
written account of the crossing, the house that teaches while writing nothing down - all treat
it as a problem rather than a rival. `cultures.unfiled` is its accidental client: a people
who hold no record and retire their names annually cannot be cited by the writ, and the only
organisation that can get them across is the one that never needed a name.
