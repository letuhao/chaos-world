# Saltwrack

A saltfolk lineage that lost its resource without anything visibly taking it.

**Why they were distinct.** A branch of the saltfolk on one evaporite basin, slightly
gracile, with a shallower brine sac — a real population difference, consistent across
every specimen, and not an artefact of what remains. They were not a subspecies in any
other respect. They were saltfolk who happened to live on one basin.

**What happened.** The brine was not drained. It was **replaced** — the basin's water
was diluted across three harvests while saltfolk were still living on it. This matters
more than it sounds, because a drain would have been visible. A dilution reads, to
anyone not watching the concentration, as a wet season. The replacement water was
potable and was poison to them. The replacement crust was food and was nutritionally
empty. Their own taste read the new brine perfectly, which nobody found useful.

**Why it went unnoticed for two generations.** Because nothing about it was dramatic.
Reproduction declined on a schedule rather than at once. Three generations. The last
was viable only as long as the old brine persisted in one sealed sump, and there is a
disputed report about how that sump was finally lost.

**What the ceiling record has to say about them.** Nothing, and this is the point worth
writing down. Their authored ceiling is ordinal 11 — the same wall every saltfolk has,
where the brine sac splits the penetrating vessel. They never reached it. They never
got past the third generation of a famine. A clade record with a ceiling it never
tested is the honest shape of most extinct records, and it is why a ceiling alone is
never evidence of a history.

**Why it is in the record at all.** Not because they were hunted. Not because anyone
found a catastrophe. **There is no event here.** One element changed concentration in
one basin, and a species that had no alternative to that one element had nothing left.
It is the cheapest extinction mechanism in the whole setting and probably the most
common, and it happens without a single identifiable villain.
