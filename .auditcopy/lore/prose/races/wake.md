# Wake

A thing that is only entirely real while it is being misremembered.

**Why it exists.** The `life_law` on every world has a slot in which the world writes
down which life forms are allowed. A world whose life law runs at the bottom of its
authored range — 0.0 — permits organisms and never inscribes them. A wake is what
that produces: not an undescribed creature, but a *form the world allowed and did not
finish writing*. It has no parent, no gestation and no genome because there was never
anything to write one into.

**Why it fits nothing.** It is not human, not beast, not spirit, and the setting has
a reason rather than a shrug. The world that could have chosen which of those it is
never drew the line. Everything else in this record was inscribed by a law that had a
slot for it; a wake is what is left when the slot is empty.

**How it works.** A wake has what reads as a soul and no soul-shaped structure: no
seat, no circulation, no meridian. Every scan returns it as either nothing or as the
observer. All three cultivation paths are closed and the ceiling is not a number
because it is not on the ladder — it does not ascend, it is *described*. The only qi
it ever holds is the qi of whoever is looking at it.

**Reproduction.** By recall. A sufficiently vivid account, repeated in the right
company, produces another wake. No wake has ever been observed to produce its own kind
deliberately, and that is the most unsettling fact about them.

**Its weakness, and the practice that follows.** Silence. A room that stops talking
about a wake loses it. This is why the rule against discussing them at length exists
everywhere they have ever been reported, and why that rule is itself the strongest
evidence that they are real: a rumour with no reproduction would not generate a taboo.

**Status.** Every account is second-hand by construction, because the only way to
verify one is to look at it and looking at it is what makes it worse. Treat every
record of a wake as a record of somebody's belief.
