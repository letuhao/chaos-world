# Longwinter

A cold-climate species that survives on a hibernate-and-store cycle.

**Why they exist.** The high steppe gives you eleven weeks of growth and nothing for
the rest of the year. The longwinter's whole body answers that arithmetic: heavy fat
storage, a lowered cold-tolerant core, a digestive system that extracts more from
frozen food than anything else alive here. They are not strong. They are the reason
anyone can cross open cold ground at all.

**What it buys.** Reach. A longwinter can walk for weeks where others stop in days,
and their body carries the stores that a party of Commonborn cannot. That single fact
makes them the setting's long-haul traders, guides and couriers regardless of what
any of them would choose to be.

**The ceiling.** Ordinal 17, the first gate needing both flank vessels pressurised at
once. A longwinter carries winter as fat and runs cold for eleven months. The flank
vessels need two warm gradients held at once, which this body manages for a few weeks
in summer and then not again for a year. They reach the seventeenth gate and lose it
every winter after.

**What it costs.** Eleven months of torpor. A hibernating longwinter is a large, warm,
immobile target and cannot be woken in time to matter. Their breakthroughs are late
and almost never fail, because qi accumulates rather than circulates — the same
mechanism that caps them is the reason they do not waste a life on a failed ascension.

**Culture, if any.** None follows from the body. Longwinter bands are crew, not
people: they take contracts, they are paid in stores, and the culture they keep is
whatever the last eleven weeks let them remember.
