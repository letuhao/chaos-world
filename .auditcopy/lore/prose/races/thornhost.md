# Thornhost

The one configuration in which the colony and the host both reach old age.

**Why it exists.** By no one's intention. A commonborn host and a thornline colony
sometimes complete integration instead of killing each other, and where it happens the
two reach old age together — which is otherwise never true of this relationship. Most
attempts end with one half winning.

**Why it is a hybrid and not a disease.** The boundary between the halves is visible
only under instrumentation, both halves are stable, and neither is dominant. That is
the definition the record uses for a hybrid: a body neither parent produces alone.

**The ceiling is the parasite's, and that is the entire legal problem as a number.**
Ordinal 8. Not because the host is weak — a commonborn could have gone on — but because
the hybrid's qi moves through the colony's tendril layer, which stops at the host's
liver. **The host could have gone on and the hybrid cannot.** Two beings, one meridian
set, and the ceiling belongs to the parasite.

**The inherited liability, in its purest form.** A thornhost has the colony's closed
mind path and the host's open instincts, fused, so the whole body answers introspection
and neither half ever gets a turn. This is the case the Thornline record means when it
says the liability is inherited rather than authored: nobody in this body did anything
and both halves paid.

**Why it is treated as one legal person.** Because it is one body, one meridian set,
and one life. Killing a thornhost kills both halves within the hour, and separating
them is known to be lethal to each. Every jurisdiction that has ruled on this has ruled
the same way, and the reason is physiology rather than sentiment — a rule that went the
other way would have to be enforced against a killing that cannot be done in halves.

**What it is for in the record.** It is the only documented case of a thornline colony
and a host both reaching old age, which makes it the reference point every other
thornline-host pair is measured against — including, and this is the point of recording
it, the pairs where nothing goes wrong.
