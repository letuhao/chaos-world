# Unwritten

A body holding more life than its world's law has room to inscribe.

**Why it exists.** Every world in this setting has a life law whose authored range is
`0.0` to `1.0`. The Unwritten is what a world produces when that law runs **above**
its ceiling: a life form granted an allowance with no slot left to write it into. It
is the setting's standing argument that the law table is a hard limit, and it is the
only record in this domain that exists to make that argument.

**How it fails, and why that is the interesting part.** Each gate an Unwritten passes
writes a fresh requirement into the body — and the body cannot satisfy it, because its
law has no slot to hold it. So each success adds a contradiction rather than removing
one. It reaches the twenty-ninth gate and no one has ever come back to describe the
thirtieth, at which point the whole accumulated contradiction resolves at once. This
record does not speculate about the result.

**The closed path is the whole species.** The mind path is closed because it cannot
tell that it is inconsistent. Observing its own state would be one more requirement it
has no slot for. Everything else — qi at more than double rate, breakthroughs at
nearly a third, every base attribute at four — is real, measured, and beside the
point.

**Status.** `unknown`, not `active`. There is one, as far as anyone can tell, the
source is a single witness account of a single event, and no propagation has ever been
observed. Every other claim about it, including the ones about what it wanted, should be
treated as the witness's.

**What it is for in this bible.** Not a monster. It is the *boundary case* that makes
the rest of the record legible: the Wake shows what a life law does at the bottom of
its range, the Echoless shows what it does when it omits a component, and the Unwritten
shows what happens when it is pushed past the top. Three records, one mechanism, and
between them the domain's own argument about why the authored ranges are ranges.
