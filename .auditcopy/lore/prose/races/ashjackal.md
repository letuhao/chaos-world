# Ashjackal

A pack quadruped that eats what qi leaves behind.

**Why they exist.** Death on the ash belt is frequent, unattended and rich. A
predator that cannot win a fight is not a predator here — it is a scavenger, and the
scavenger niche in a world this violent is not thin but permanent. The ashjackal is
built for it: fast, unspecialised, able to read a qi gradient through falling ash from
far enough away to arrive after everyone else has left.

**What it costs.** Everything that requires standing still. They cannot take a healthy
adult of anything, which is why they are removed from a live fight in under a minute,
and they are the setting's clearest example of a life built entirely around not being
present.

**Two closed paths.** The mind path closes because a heat-exchange brainplate has no
cortical folding for introspection to work on, and the body path closes because qi
cannot be stored in a frame built to shed heat. The qi path is the only one open, and
it is fast — they absorb residual qi out of ash and carcass at a rate nothing else
matches.

**The ceiling.** Ordinal 8, the last of the ordinary meridians, and for a structural
reason rather than a metabolic one: a four-legged frame carries its mass on the
forelimbs and has no hindquarter girth with which to close the girdling vessel. They
reach the liver gate and then there is no body left to close.

**What it is not.** A pack is a kinship by scent, not a family. It dissolves when the
scent changes. Do not read a pack as a polity, a culture or a species' social form —
it is a foraging strategy that happens to have members.
