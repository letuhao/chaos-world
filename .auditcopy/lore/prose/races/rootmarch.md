# Rootmarch

A sap-driven body that flowers.

**Why they exist.** Every other body here stores wealth in muscle, in qi, or in a
colony. A rootmarch stores it in seed, and seed survives a famine that kills a grain
store. That is the whole reason: a plant lineage is the only one in the record that
holds a harvest across the bad years, and it was engineered upward from a
mycorrhizal food crop specifically for that.

**The tradeoff.** Wood is not muscle. The body path is closed outright — there is no
tissue to temper, and no amount of technique changes that. They cannot fight and they
do not try to. Their whole body is built for one long argument about soil.

**The ceiling.** Ordinal 4. The yin-organ gate needs a channel running between
distinct pairs of organs. A rootmarch's three sap columns branch but do not pair —
there is no heart analogue and no kidney analogue, and therefore no channel to close.
It clears the gate set on stored sap alone and stops there. A rootmarch that is told
it can ascend past the fourth realm has been told something anatomically false.

**Reproduction.** Seed, and seed is safe. The dangerous part of a rootmarch life is
establishment, not conception: the fruit must drop in soil it can root into, and it
cannot move once it has.

**What they are good for.** A rootmarch can be sealed in a dark field and live for
months. In a famine that makes it the most valuable thing in the region. In a war
that makes it a hostage, and the species has no ability to refuse.

**Culture, if any.** None that the body imposes. Rootmarch are farmed, and a farm
does not have a culture.
