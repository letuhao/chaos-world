# Cinderkin

An elemental body, and a law slot it cannot do without.

**Why they exist.** They are not descended from anything. A cinderkin exists where a
world's elemental law slot resolves to fire and something in that world metabolises
it. That is the whole origin story, and it has a mechanical consequence worth stating
plainly: **a world with no elemental law slot has no cinderkin.** The mortal tier
carries three law slots and no room for an elemental body, which is why the species
is immortal- and transcendent-world only, and why any claim of a cinderkin on a
mortal world is either a lie or a different thing wearing the name.

**What the range buys.** The elemental law's authored range is 0.0 to 1.0, not a
switch. A world that spends its slot on cold produces a different body entirely. The
cinderkin population tracks the world's slot, not any birth rate.

**Reproduction.** None. It does not have young; it has pressure. Under enough stress a
cinderkin sheds a spark that finds fuel and re-constitutes on its own. This is why the
population moves like weather and why no one has ever successfully counted one.

**The ceiling.** There isn't one, and there is no gate to fail. A cinderkin's qi has
no organ to sit in — the fire *is* the body — so qi passes through it continuously
instead of being stored. Every gate that asks for storage is answered instantly. The
body path is closed because there is no tissue to temper; a cinderkin that attempts it
disperses and re-forms smaller, which is recorded as the clearest proof in the record
that a body and an element are not the same kind of thing.

**What they killed.** The Glasskin. Vitrified bodies do not burn, but a cinderkin is
not a flame around a body, it is a body, and it does not distinguish glass from any
other fuel when it is hungry enough. That single interaction destroyed an entire
species, and it is the reason anyone now treats an element as a process.
