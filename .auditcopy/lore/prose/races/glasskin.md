# Glasskin

A human lineage that stopped ageing by stopping being tissue.

**Why they existed.** They began as an attempt to answer a question almost nobody in
the record asked properly: what if a body did not decay? The lineage under selection
toward stillness — the same mistake, in the end, as asking a body not to age. A
progressive silicate replacement came up from the extremities inward, and at full
maturity a glasskin was a standing cast in human proportions.

**What it bought.** An authored lifespan of ten thousand years and complete immunity
to qi — no meridian, no circulation, no dantian, because glass does not conduct.
Nothing that kills a cultivator touches one. They were the most durable bodies in the
record.

**What it cost.** Everything that requires a person. Glass does not temper, so the body
path closed. Glass does not conduct, so the qi path closed. And the tissue that would
have done the introspecting was precisely the tissue that got replaced, so the mind
path closed too. There were no senses after vitrification, no appetite, and no
reported distress — and no way, ever, to ask one whether that was a preference.

**The end.** Each generation vitrified later than the last and fertility fell with it,
until the last cohort vitrified before it could breed. Then the world's physical law
was retuned upward and material hardness rose — and every body that had been
strengthened by the old value failed at once. Not one at a time. All of them,
together, in a single afternoon, on one mountain.

**What the record is actually arguing.** The Glasskin are the strongest available
argument in this domain that **a body is not a container for a person**. Their souls
are present, inert, and read as sitting inside a body. Any account of the last cohort
that describes them as having been content, or as having been imprisoned, is making a
claim about people that the evidence cannot reach — and the clade's destruction is a
documented moral problem for exactly that reason rather than a solved question.

**Two failures, one lesson.** They died of a law change they could not perceive, in a
body built to be unkillable, which nobody in the record had thought to check against
the one thing a body has no answer for: a rule changing.
