# Elemental Deficiency

## What is absent

`elemental_law`, in any mortal-tier world. Its authored description: "Governs dominant
elements, elemental balance, and seasonal cycles." All three of its jobs stop.

## Why it is unwritten rather than weak

`elemental_law.tier_ids` lists all four tiers. It is *available* in the mortal tier. It is
simply not *imprinted*, because the three mortal slots are already pinned:

| slot | spent on | what needs it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `physical_law` | `iron_ore` — hardness, weight, gravity |
| 2 | `life_law` | `herb_common` — a plant is alive |
| 3 | `qi_law` | `ironhide_bear` at combat power 5 |

Nothing in that table needs an element. An element is not a hardness, a metabolism or a
stored charge, so it is the group a small budget drops first.

This is the distinction the whole batch turns on: **unwritten is not the same as
unavailable**. A strained law is present at the floor of its range and can be pushed. An
unwritten law has no dial at all.

## The four physical consequences

**Seasons drift instead of turning.** Elemental law governs seasonal cycles. Unwritten,
the year is weather: it recurs, but nothing holds it to a cycle, and a region with no
authored seasonal cause inherits a climate that changes for reasons of its own.

**Ore carries no affinity.** `iron_ore` is ore. Without an elemental imprint, a material
has no elemental share to be struck at, so an elemental treasure cannot form in this tier
and cannot be forged toward one from here.

**No elemental life form.** An elemental is a life form, and nothing is licensing one.
`storm_elemental` lists `immortal_world` and `transcendent_world` only, with combat power
100 – 1000 — it does not exist below the tier whose budget first allows it.

**Elemental damage has nothing to act on.** This is the sharpest cost for a practitioner.
The realm may be reachable. The effect is not. An elemental technique is learnable anywhere
and usable nowhere in this tier, and a failure here is not a mistake of aim.

## What would fix it

Writing the imprint costs a slot and then costs upkeep every period after. A mortal-tier
world's `upkeep_rate` band is 0.1 – 0.5, the lowest in the setting. The world that has the
least qi is the one that can least afford the group that would let it do more.

Its counterpart at the other end of the ladder is **Elemental Emergence**: the same group,
concentrated, at the immortal tier.
