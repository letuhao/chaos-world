# The Three-Dao Split

## Why not one

A unified Dao would have to read **all six groups together, at once**. That is not a
question of conviction — it is a budget. Six legible groups need a layer's share of the
budget to hold all six at working values, and the only tier whose budget does that is
`transcendent_world` at fifteen slots across three layers.

So one Dao is not heresy or arrogance. It is arithmetic, and it is available only at the
top of the ladder, to whoever can already hold fifteen imprints.

## Why three and not two

Three is the **largest number of coherent readings a six-slot spirit-tier world can
hold**, because each reading needs its own three-group subset and the subsets must not
compete for the same slots.

Two would fit easily. Two would also leave a spirit-tier world's six slots with room to
spare, and a reading with room to spare is not a reading — it is a preference.

## Why not four

A fourth Dao would need a fifth group read **on its own**. There is no fifth group. The
six `WorldLawDef` resources are the entire vocabulary of the setting, so the number of
self-sufficient readings is bounded by the vocabulary, not by history, invention or the
number of people who disagreed.

This is why adding a fourth Dao faction is not a content request. It is a request to
extend the authored law vocabulary, which is a different and much larger change.

## The three subsets

| Dao | reads | reading |
|---|---|---|
| Body | physical + life + spatial | form persists; preservation through form |
| Qi | qi + temporal + physical | energy is the medium; creation through energy |
| Mind | spatial + temporal + qi | the self exceeds its container; transcendence through spirit |

Note the structure. Body and Qi overlap on exactly one group. Qi and Mind overlap on two.
Body and Mind overlap on exactly one — and use it in opposite directions.

## The asymmetry is the point

Body and Qi are **neutral**: they share `physical_law` as a medium and neither reading
denies the other. A practitioner of either can stand in a world built for the other.

Qi and Mind are **allied**: they share `qi_law` and `temporal_law`, so both need a
reservoir and a clock, and both are cheap where spirit qi is dense.

Body and Mind are **hostile**: they share `spatial_law` and read it oppositely — as a
container that holds against an exit the self leaves by. One faction says the world is
what the perfect form endures *inside*. The other says the world is what refining the
self leaves *behind*.

They cannot both be right, and the setting gives them no room to be approximately right.
That hostility is structural, authored into the law set, and older than either faction.

## What is left over

The mortal tier has three slots and cannot hold any Dao. The immortal tier has ten and is
nobody's home. Only `spirit_world` is home, native and staffed simultaneously — which is
why **Qi Dao** is the only Dao with a population that has never had to migrate.
