# Elemental Emergence

## The claim

An elemental is the first life form that needs **three law groups written into one body at
once** — element, mass, motion. Elemental, physical, qi.

So an elemental cannot appear until a layer's share of a world's budget can gather all
three in one place.

## Why not the spirit tier

`spirit_world` holds all six groups. That is not sufficient.

Six imprints have to cover a volume of 100 – 1000 units, so the surface layer must spread
them. The groups exist; they are not concentrated. An elemental is a body that needs them
together, and a body is a small place.

`immortal_world` holds ten imprints over at least two layers — the surplus is arithmetic,
since ten over six leaves four duplicates and a duplicate needs somewhere to sit. Ten
slots let three groups be written into one layer at working values.

**That is the tier where elemental life becomes arithmetically possible, and it is the tier
the authored data already names:** `storm_elemental` lists `immortal_world` and
`transcendent_world` only, at `combat_power_min` 100.0 — the tier where it appears is also
the tier where its budget jumps.

## Why this matters beyond a category

**Elementals are the first inhabitants a law budget can create on its own.** Everything
else in the setting is either a plant, a beast or a humanoid whose existence is assumed.
An elemental is the one life form that is *derived from the law composition* rather than
merely listed alongside it.

Which is why `immortal_court` staffs itself with `["humanoid", "elemental"]` instead of
hiring: an elemental is not a population to be recruited, it is a consequence of holding
ten slots.

## The consequence on both sides

The budget that produces elementals also produces the scarcity. `immortal_court` has
`danger_level` 8, and the reason a court is dangerous is not that it is well defended —
it is that the law concentration which makes its elementals possible is the same
concentration that makes anything formed there violently stable.

## The counterpart

`cosmology.elemental_deficiency` is the same group at the bottom of the ladder: unwritten,
in a world whose three slots are pinned by ore, herbs and a beast's strength.

Together they are the batch's whole claim about tiers: **elemental life is a function of
law concentration, not of altitude.**
