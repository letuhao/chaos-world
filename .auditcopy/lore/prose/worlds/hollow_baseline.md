# The Hollow Baseline

## Three seats, spent

The frame nobody had to choose: `physical_law` at 1.4, `life_law` at 0.62, `qi_law` on the
floor at 0.1. Six groups exist. Three are written. That is the whole content of the lowest
tier, and every settlement on the plains is a consequence of which three those were.

The choice was not free in either direction. `physical_law` because ore has hardness and
weight. `life_law` because a herb is alive. `qi_law` because an Ironhide Bear reaches five
and not zero, and a beast that hits for five is running on stored qi. Spend anywhere else and
the content stops existing.

## What three vacancies cost

**Elemental unwritten.** No season runs. Nothing on this ground is ever dormant, so
`herb_common` regrows until the grazing pressure stops it and not one moment longer - which is
why a bad grazing year is a medical shortage before it is a hunger, and why the one herb is
also the feed and the calories. It is also why a furnace body here has no budget to spend
into: fire is combustion, and every discharge is paid in flesh.

**Spatial unwritten.** Distance is not conserved. No two surveys of the same ground have ever
agreed, so possession is proven by a marker standing and never by measuring where it stands.
This is the Hollow Measure, and it is why no deed, tax boundary or march order here can be
enforced by measurement. It is also why the seventy-four unit size is a **ceiling**: nothing
presses the frame open, it is born at a size and it stays there, which is the physical reason
this world cannot become a spirit world.

**Temporal unwritten.** No clock. The flood on the Widow Run is the only cycle the ground
offers, so a festival hangs off a measured flood post rather than a date, a contract runs to
the flood, and a settlement on the watercourse is revocable once a generation.

## The seat doing two jobs

`life_law` at 0.62 is the most expensive thing here. That one imprint has to keep plants and
beasts coherent enough for an herb to be medicine and a bear to be a problem - and it has
nothing left over. That is not a policy; it is the arithmetic. The tier admits `plant` and
`beast` and nothing else, so **no humanoid is admissible**.

So every practitioner standing on the Cairn Field is a visitor. The ring exists because those
nine trials are the only thing anybody would walk far to watch, which means the plains' one
export is an audience, which means gate fees are the only tax this world can actually
collect. The whole economy - the brokers selling standing, the office certifying readings it
cannot perform from inside, the watch keeping the queue on the one door, the extraction guilds
hiring bodies for work the tier will not license anyone to do - is the invoice for a single
imprint spent at 0.62.

## The cheapest frame in the setting

Upkeep 0.22 against 7.4 at the top. The least expensive world anyone can hold is the one
that could not afford a fourth seat, and every later tier is a receipt for somebody else's
payment.
