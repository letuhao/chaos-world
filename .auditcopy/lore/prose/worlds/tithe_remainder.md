# The Tithe Remainder

## Six seats, six groups

The first budget that can hold the whole vocabulary. Nothing in this frame is merely missing:
physical 2.1, spatial 1.3, qi 4.6, temporal 0.9, elemental 0.45, life 0.71.

Four things follow immediately, and each of them is a place or an institution elsewhere in the
setting:

- A **season** exists, because elemental law is written. So a pass can have a safe window, a
  sea can be crossed on a date, and a trial can be scheduled rather than merely attempted.
- A **line** exists, because spatial law is written. So a border can be surveyed and defended
  rather than asserted, which is what the Threshold Builders are paid for.
- A **humanoid** is licensed, for the first time anywhere. Every humanoid practitioner in the
  setting begins at this tier.
- A **cycle** exists, because temporal law is written. A person's own lifespan is now a
  measurable discount against the world's clock.

## The cost of having all of it

Nothing is vacant, and that is the constraint. Six imprints have to cover 100 to 1000 units of
ground, so no single body gathers three groups in one place. An elemental is three groups in
one shell - holding all three is not the same as gathering them - so elementals wait. The
tier that first loses nothing still cannot afford one elemental.

It shows up at the other end too. A maximally dense fixed body stops at realm seventeen here,
one gate short of the end of its own tier, because there is no duplicated group left to open
the cavity it needs. `history.consequence_the_density_ceiling` has been unexplained since: it
is a composition result. Nothing was failed.

## What it is for

This frame is the whole of the trade that the upper tiers feed on. Jade holds qi and cannot be
found below this tier. Spirit stone cannot be found below it. The mortals who climbed the stair
came for both, and everything above - the crossings, the tariffs, the counting houses, the
paperless people who would not be priced - is downstream of what is in the ground here.
