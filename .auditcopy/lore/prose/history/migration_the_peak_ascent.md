# The Peak Ascent

- **Push.** No iron. See the Iron Grief.
- **Pull.** Spirit stone and jade ore, both of which exist only in the spirit tier. They cannot be
  carried down and smuggled; they are not found at the bottom.
- **Direction of travel.** Up, and permanently. Nobody in this era is recorded as descending the
  Peak Ascent to resettle the plains.

The migration is the reason the mortal tier's later eras are quiet. The people who would have
replaced the lost miners left instead, so a single exhausted seam cost the tier a generation as
well as a ladder.

It is also the first time the setting's geography, not its politics, decided where people lived.
Every later era inherits that precedence.
