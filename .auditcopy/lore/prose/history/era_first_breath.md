# The First Breath

Realms one and two. The oldest era, and the only one whose problem is purely arithmetic.

- **Why it begins.** Holding a world costs upkeep, and a mortal world costs 0.1 to 0.5. It is
  the cheapest frame that can be sealed at all, so it is the frame that got sealed first.
- **Why it is short.** Qi law has a floor of 0.1 and a mortal world seats three of six laws.
  Enough qi exists to light one breath and hold one form. There is no surplus to expand into.
- **What the era produced.** The habit of treating cultivation as a fuel budget, which every
  later era inherits and none of them manage to unlearn.

The name is literal. The first thing anyone learned was that a single breath is a resource, and
that the world will not supply a second one for free.
