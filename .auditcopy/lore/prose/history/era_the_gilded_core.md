# The Gilded Core

Realms three and four: Core Formation and Nascent Soul.

- **Why it is named after metal.** A core needs a cage. Iron ore on the Mortal Plains is the only
  dense material the mortal tier lists, so every core in the tier was mined from the same kind of
  ground for the same reason.
- **The dependency it created.** A spiritual ladder became a supply chain. The third gate stopped
  being about cultivation and started being about how deep you had to dig.
- **Why it ended.** It ended at the seam. See the Iron Grief.

Nothing in this era is mysterious. It is the first era where the cause of everybody's ceiling is
a hole in the ground.
