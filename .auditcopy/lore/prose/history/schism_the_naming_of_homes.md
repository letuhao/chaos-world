# The Naming of Homes

The event that fixed the three paths' tiers, and the event that made the fix permanent.

- **What was decided.** One home tier per path: body to the mortal tier, qi to the spirit tier,
  mind to the transcendent tier.
- **The reasoning, which was correct.** A method belongs where it works. Each path was handed the
  frame it had been practising in.
- **The error.** The naming was recorded as a preference. From then on the setting described the
  body path as having chosen confinement rather than as being unable to afford anything else, and
  nobody costed a tier again.
- **What the frames actually are.** The mortal tier is the only composition whose three seated laws
  keep material hardness and energy density constant, which is the only frame in which a body can
  be certified. Above it, with six or ten or fifteen seats, the physical law's own range of 0.1 to
  10.0 means the body is refined against laws that move, and nothing is ever perfect.
- **The consequence.** The body path's own promise, that a perfect form endures beyond worlds, is
  unkeepable by the method that makes it. It is not a doctrine it lost. It is a bill it cannot
  stop being charged.

The Dao stances in force were never disputed by this decree and were not produced by it: body and
qi were indifferent, mind and body were structurally opposed, qi and mind were allied. What the
decree did was attach a place to each of them, which is what turned a working arrangement into a
fate.
