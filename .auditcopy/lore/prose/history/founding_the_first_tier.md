# The Seating of the First Tier

The founding act, and it is an economic act rather than a creative one.

- **The bill.** A tier costs upkeep to hold. The curve runs 0.1-0.5 (mortal), 0.5-1.0 (spirit),
  1.0-5.0 (immortal), 5.0-20.0 (transcendent). Roughly fortyfold across the whole ladder.
- **The currency.** Qi law is the only law whose value range spans a thousandfold (0.1 to 100.0).
  Every other law is flatter. Qi is therefore the only thing that can be paid in bulk.
- **The consequence.** The opener bought seats. They bought three. The frame they could afford was
  exactly the frame that exists at the bottom of the ladder, and its three seats fixed what a
  mortal world would forever be able to do.
- **What is missing.** No record in this bible names the payer. The bill survived; the signature
  did not. That gap is not a lost detail, it is the reason the three-tier settlement looks like a
  law of nature instead of a decision somebody made.

Everything above the mortal tier is a later instalment on the same debt.
