# The Unowned Genealogy

An open question that survives because it is arithmetic, not because it is secret.

- **The fact.** One line has no recorded origin and no household that will claim it. Its founding
  bar sits at a height that the arithmetic says exactly one generation of pure-by-pure inheritance
  can clear. Anything weaker is unobtainable by any pairing.
- **The obvious reading, and why it is not accepted.** It looks like the product of the Branch
  Cull: narrowed enough to cross the bar, unattributable because a cull deliberately destroys the
  record of what was culled. That would make this line a debt somebody else's descendants are
  still paying.
- **The competing claim.** The oldest surviving carrier record belongs to a different line, kept in
  one household for so long that the name stopped describing a family. It cannot prove it is older,
  because its own record begins after the culls.
- **Why it stays open.** Two records, one of them arithmetic and one of it a memory, and no
  authority in the setting is entitled to decide between them.

This is a mystery with a first-class shape and no owner. It is recorded rather than resolved
because resolving it would mean inventing a descent nobody can corroborate.
