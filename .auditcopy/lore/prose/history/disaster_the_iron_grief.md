# The Iron Grief

The first catastrophe, and the one that proves the seat budget is a physical limit rather than a
philosophical one.

- **The trigger.** A seam ran out.
- **Why nobody could follow it.** Opening a new seam means changing how much rock there is and
  where it sits. That is spatial work, and the mortal tier seats three laws out of six: physical,
  qi and life. Spatial is applicable to the tier and installed nowhere in it. The mountain cannot
  be persuaded to move.
- **The consequence.** Cores stopped forming. A realm that needs a caged medium and has no medium
  is not a hard realm, it is an absent one. Nascent Soul went with it.
- **Why this is a disaster and not a famine.** Everyone above Foundation was already invested. The
  loss was not of the people who would have been born; it was of the people already standing.

The grief is still legible: the worked-out ground on the Mortal Plains is the oldest continuously
abandoned infrastructure in the setting, and it is abandoned because the tier cannot afford to dig.
