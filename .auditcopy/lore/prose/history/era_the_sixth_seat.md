# The Sixth Seat

Realms ten and eleven: Spirit Condensation and Spirit Sea.

- **What makes it an era.** Six seats, six laws. It is the first composition in which nothing is
  applicable-but-absent. Every tier below it was missing three laws; every tier above it has spare
  seats. This one is exactly right for the medium and no better.
- **Why it was unavoidable.** Spirit stone and jade ore cannot be found below the spirit tier, so
  the composition that can use them is the composition that gets built.
- **Why it is a boundary and not a golden age.** Completeness with zero redundancy is brittle. A
  six-seat tier holding six laws has no seat to lose.

The phrase "the sixth seat" is the whole argument in two words. A seat is not a feature. It is a
position in a composition that has to be paid for.
