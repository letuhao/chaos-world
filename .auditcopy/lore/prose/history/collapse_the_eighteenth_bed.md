# The Eighteenth Bed Collapse

- **What was attempted.** A permanent crossing. Somebody wanted a road.
- **Why a road cannot be a road.** Upkeep is charged per period, not once. A crossing held by an
  institution is a crossing whose upkeep the institution must pay forever, at the immortal tier's
  rate. The rate is between one and five units per period for every seat in the bed.
- **What is left.** A bed of seats that were opened and never released. They are installed, and
  nothing is in them, and they cannot be closed because closing them would require paying the same
  arrears.
- **The rule that came out of it.** No sect holds the crossing. Any body crosses on its own
  account, or does not cross.

The collapse is still visible as infrastructure: the most expensive set of unusable seats in the
setting, and a standing prohibition that every organisation in this bible still observes.
