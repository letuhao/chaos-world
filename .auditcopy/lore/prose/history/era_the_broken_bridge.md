# The Broken Bridge

One realm, and the cheapest era name in the timeline for the most expensive gate.

- **The number.** Upkeep at the spirit tier is 0.5 to 1.0. At the immortal tier it is 1.0 to 5.0.
  The border multiplies the price of standing still by up to ten.
- **Why the gate broke instead of being expensive.** A gate you can afford once is a gate. A gate
  you have to keep affording forever is a gate that only institutions can hold, and no institution
  in this bible could afford one continuously. See the Eighteenth Bed.
- **Who never arrived.** Most of the setting. The Realm Nine Verdict put the commonest bodies one
  gate below the mortal ceiling, so the majority of cultivators had two sealed tiers between
  themselves and this border and no way to know it was there.

The bridge is broken for the ordinary reason anything expensive is inaccessible: the price is not
the difficulty, it is the running cost.
