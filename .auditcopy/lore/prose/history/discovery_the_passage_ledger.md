# The Passage Ledger

- **The observation.** Movement between tiers costs upkeep, and upkeep scales with seat count and
  tier. Fortyfold per tier, four tiers. A body that intends to stand anywhere above the mortal
  tier is carrying a running cost forever.
- **The consequence.** Only a body can be moved. Resources can be sold but not moved, because they
  belong to the tier that seats them. So the only real trade in this setting is people moving, and
  the only thing anyone can actually control is whether a given body is permitted to move.
- **Why that produced counting houses.** A house that writes down who may pass, at what rate, on
  whose word, is selling the only scarce good there is. Two of them now write the contract and
  price it, which is a rivalry rather than a monopoly.
- **Why it produced a marshal.** Authority over passage is the one authority that needs no body of
  its own. The Marshal may close the crossings and call the levy, and the levy is answered by
  people who may say no.

The rule that follows from all of it, and that everything else in this bible depends on: nobody may
levy a seat. You can be charged for passing. You cannot be charged for existing.
