# The Refused Seating

- **The attempt.** A vacant seat, and a proposal to give it a seventh law. The reasoning was not
  stupid: the upper tiers have slots, so a law was needed.
- **Why it fails.** A seat holds a law. There were six. A seventh seat given a seventh name holds
  the absence of the seventh law, and consumes its own slot doing it.
- **The irreversible part.** A consumed slot and an empty seat are indistinguishable from outside
  the ledger. Both report no law installed. The ledger stopped accepting additions because after
  this it could not tell a refusal from an oversight.
- **Why it is the most consequential thing in this file.** It is why the setting knows six laws
  and not seven, and why the vacant seats of the upper tiers cannot be certified empty. Every
  unfilled seat above is now a question rather than an opportunity.

Nobody has attempted it since. That is recorded as a number, not as a virtue.
