# The Cinder Famine

- **The mechanism.** Fire affinity here is structural, not cultivated. Affinity needs a seated law
  to draw on. Elemental law is applicable to every tier and seated in the mortal and spirit ones
  only in part, so a fire body in a mortal or spirit frame has no elemental budget, and an
  affinity that cannot be fed will eat its own substrate.
- **What that predicts.** The fastest refiner should have the shortest life. It does. It also
  should be the most numerous, because a body that supplies its own fuel never queues at a seam.
  It is.
- **Why it is a famine and not a plague.** Nothing broke. The population simply ran down, at a
  rate set by how fast it could live, which is the rate at which it died.
- **The residue.** Dominance high, lifespan half of anything else, and a grant that trades impact
  for speed. All three authored facts fall out of one missing seat.

Nothing here needed a villain. An underfunded tier plus a structural affinity plus a population
that grew to consume the arrangement is a sufficient famine.
