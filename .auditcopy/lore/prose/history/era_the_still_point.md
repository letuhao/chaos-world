# The Still Point

Three realms: Transcendent, Dao Ancestor, Primordial Origin.

- **The slack.** Fifteen seats against six laws. Nine vacant, and unlike the immortal tier's four
  these are too many to be an administrative detail.
- **What slack buys.** Reading the Sixth Seat's finding, applied: a law can be isolated and
  inspected only where there is a spare seat to isolate it into. Nine spares is the difference
  between obeying six laws and studying them.
- **The time.** Ten to a hundred times faster. A single held thought outlasts any institution in
  this bible, and that is not mysticism, it is a rate.
- **Why it is a still point and not a summit.** Nothing happens there. Capacity that cannot be
  spent is the definition of a still point, and the era's defining event is a refusal rather than
  an arrival.

The highest tier of the setting is also its least eventful, and the reason is that nothing about
it can be spent.
