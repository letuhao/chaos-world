# The Deep Caverns

The lightless karst and lava tube network beneath the Spirit Peaks: saturated air, near-constant
humidity, and no daylight at any depth.

A body that lost its eyes first and negotiated its symbiosis second can live here and nowhere
else. Dry, sunlit or vibrating ground kills the colony within a single season and the body goes
with it; fire ends it quickly. What the caverns supply is not shelter but three specific
conditions, and a body outside all three is not uncomfortable, it is dead inside a year.

There is no horizon down here. That is not a metaphor and it is the reason a question went
unasked for a very long time.

The cavern population reads surfaces by the pattern of fungal response a hand makes on the wall -
it emits light and cannot receive it, so a wall is legible to it the way a face is legible from
across a room, and only to somebody who already knows how to look. Their souls are held very
deep and very quiet, which makes them difficult to read and exceptionally hard to damage.

Above ground, a lineage of that population lost its colony strain, and a mineral bloom able to
survive the same conditions colonised the wound instead. The body grew around what replaced the
fungus rather than recovering from it. It sees in the dark. It lives ninety years instead of two
hundred and fifty, and it is far more fertile.

The substitution is the single most useful finding anyone has made down here, because it proves
the closed path was the symbiosis and not the body plan: the mind channel reopened in the
population that got rid of the fungus. Nothing had ever been able to test that, because there was
nothing to compare the cavern population against.

Now there is. That comparison is the only reason this record exists.
