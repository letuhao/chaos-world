# Immortal Court

The Immortal Court is the third tier's anchor: a high district of cut terraces over the Ember Ley and the Starmetal Scar, worked by humanoids and elementals together.

It exists because ten law slots over six groups leaves four duplicate imprints, and a duplicate needs somewhere to sit - so this is the first tier with a second layer, and the surplus law has to rise through the district rather than being argued about above it.

What makes it worth holding is the surplus itself: the Overlayer Shelf above the court is the only place in the setting where a law is written twice and the second writing is the stronger one, and every office on the nine terraces is priced by how close it sits to that shelf.

The tier runs fast. Five to fifty times a slower world's clock means a scar in the ground heals before the ledger that recorded it has been copied, and it means the star metal renews faster than it can be cut. Nothing in this district is exhausted, which is either a blessing or an accounting error depending on who is doing the accounts.

Elemental law is written here and written twice, so fire on the Ember Ley is not a technique being attempted - it is fire behaving as intended for the first time in the setting's history. Every fire practitioner who ever reached this tier came here for one reason, and the ley is the reason.
