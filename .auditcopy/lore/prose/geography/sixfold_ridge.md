# Sixfold Ridge

The folded range the Spirit Peaks are the name of: one wall carrying spirit stone and jade ore in the same bedding, folded six times, which is why no single owner has ever held both.

It exists because the spirit tier writes all six law groups, so the ridge can be surveyed end to end and its two deposits read as one body of ground rather than as two claims that happen to be near each other.

What makes it worth fighting over is the geology: every claim on stone is a claim on jade and every claim on jade is a claim on the pass, so there is no version of this range that can be settled one resource at a time.

A range that has been surveyed is a different object from a range that has been claimed. On the mortal plains a boundary is a belief; here it is a line somebody walked and wrote down, and the line is the same length for everyone who goes and looks.

The six folds are why the pass matters. Six crossings would be six arguments; one crossing is one bottleneck, and a bottleneck is a place where a small number of people can decide a large number of things.
