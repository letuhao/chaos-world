# The Floodplain Margin

The band of ground beside the Widow Run where flood water stands after the channel has emptied,
and the only ground on the Mortal Plains with standing shallow water through the dry season.

The distinction between this and the river is the whole point. The run is the plains' single
clock, and a clock that runs a few weeks a year is not a habitat - it is an event. Everything
that lives on the plains' water has to be somewhere the water *stays*, and the water stays in
the margin: the flat ground the flood left behind when it went back into its channel.

That band is re-made every flood. The run moves its bed far enough between floods to relocate
every settlement on it, and it relocates the margin with it, so this is not a place anybody
holds. It is a shape the river keeps arriving at.

The eggs of the margin's own people are laid in a woven reed mat and carried under a brood pouch
through the dry months, hatched when the water returns. Fertilisation is external and needs
standing water, so conception happens where this is and not where it is convenient. Skin
desiccates fast out of the water, so travel is timed to the flood as strictly as the hatching is.

This is why the margin supports the plains' one large migratory herd and why the herd returns on
a cycle nobody on the ground has predicted. Both the herd and the people here are waiting on the
same water, and both are wrong about the date.
