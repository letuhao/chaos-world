# Spirit Peaks

The Spirit Peaks are the second tier's anchor: one folded ridge carrying spirit stone and jade ore in the same bedding, inhabited by beasts and humanoids together.

They exist because six law slots is the first budget that can hold all six law groups, which means this is the first tier where a coast holds its shape, a season keeps to a cycle and a distance means the same thing twice - so the ridge can be walked to, divided and held.

What makes them worth holding is that the two resources are one geological accident: every claim on spirit stone is a claim on jade and every claim on jade is a claim on the pass, which is the only way over. Three resources, one wall, one door.

This is where the setting acquires the idea of a border. Everything below this tier is a claim, and everything above it is an interior; here, for the first time, a line of stone can be walked from end to end by two people who disagree about it, and the walk is the argument.

The tier's calendar is a resource in its own right. Elemental law is written, so the Tidemark season keeps to a cycle, the crossing window can be scheduled, and whoever reads the mark sets the dates. A shipping date obeyed by people who have never heard of its author is the earliest form of law in the setting.
