# Void Expanse

The cured end of the shelf: where dao_fragment has finished breaking down into void crystal, in a quantity no lower tier's physics can hold.

It exists because curing is one-way and runs at ten to a hundred times a slower world's clock, so the expanse grows every period and nothing anybody does can slow it.

That makes it the only resource in the setting with a guaranteed supply - and therefore the only one nobody can corner, which puts its holders in the strange position of being the only people in the setting with nothing to gain from a war.

Every scarcity argument in the setting is downstream of a resource that could run out. This is the one that cannot. The expanse is what a fragment becomes when nobody stops it, and the tier's clock does not offer anybody the chance.

So the holders of the void crystal are rich in the only currency that matters and have no claim on anything else. A region that cannot be monopolised is a region nobody defends, which is why the expeditions that work it are the setting's only ventures run by people who are not defending territory.
