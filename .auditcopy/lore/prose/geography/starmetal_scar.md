# Starmetal Scar

Where star metal surfaces in the Immortal Court: a wound in the ground that keeps healing because this tier runs at five to fifty times a slower world's clock.

It exists because immortal essence is drawn from what the scar keeps producing, so the strongest material in the setting renews faster than it can be mined.

That is the whole problem: a resource that cannot be exhausted cannot be monopolised either, so the scar is indefensible ground that everyone must keep using, and the only durable claim on it is over the approach rather than over the metal.

A self-renewing deposit is either the ideal commons or the worst property dispute in the setting, and it turns out to be the second one. Because the metal keeps coming, nobody ever has to leave, which means nobody ever has to sell, which means the land stays in the hands of whoever was there when it started.

So the scar is inherited rather than earned, and the arguments are not about the metal - it cannot run out - but about the assays, the roads and the shelter, which are the only parts of the operation that can actually be contested.
