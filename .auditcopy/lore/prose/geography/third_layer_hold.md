# Third Layer Hold

The Transcendent Realm's third layer, where fifteen law imprints over three layers leave nine surplus imprints with nowhere to go but up.

It exists because at this tier the surplus is not a cost, it is the place: nine laws written more than once is what a body standing here is measured against, and standing here is the privilege.

What makes it worth holding is that nothing about it is survivable casually - nothing grows, nothing recovers, nothing is cheap - so access is a discipline rather than a journey, and the tier's entire social ladder is a queue for it.

At the bottom tier the surplus is a hole in the ground and nobody notices. At the top tier the surplus is nine laws, laid out in the air, and everybody notices. The same arithmetic reads as waste at three slots and as a hierarchy at fifteen.

This is also why the top tier is assembled rather than grown. Nine surplus imprints is more than one will can hold, so the ground exists because a merge put it here, and every polity on the Fragmentshelf above is living on somebody else's merger and knows it.
