# Transcendent Realm

The Transcendent Realm is the top tier's anchor: a shattered shelf of broken law where dao_fragment cures into void crystal, held by humanoids and elementals under a three-layer imprint set.

It exists because fifteen imprints over three layers leave nine surplus imprints, and nine surplus imprints is not a resource but a load - so this tier is built or merged and never grown into, and the ground it stands on is made of what was left over.

What makes it worth holding is the one door in the setting: the Rift Shore below it is the only ground where two worlds have ever come adjacent, because the top budget is the only one with enough imprints gathered close enough to push one off the end of its range.

Two of the tier's resources are two stages of the same wound. A dao fragment is a broken piece of a law; left alone it cures, and a cured fragment is void crystal. The shelf is therefore worked hardest by whoever understands that it will be worthless in a generation and contested hardest by everyone who does not.

The void expanse grows. Curing runs at ten to a hundred times a slower world's clock and cannot be slowed, so the one resource in the setting with a guaranteed supply is also the one nobody can corner - which makes its holders the only people in the setting with nothing to gain from a war.
