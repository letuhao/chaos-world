# Vacant Elemental Ground

The ground effect at the two elemental domains where elemental law is unspent: a technique at full strength producing nothing at all, which is the signature absence leaves rather than weakness.

It exists because an unwritten law group has three signatures - no process runs on it, no substance carries it, no life form is licensed by it - and an elemental technique needs all three. It runs. It is pure. It does nothing.

That is what makes the Ember Domain and the Tide Domain the setting's most instructive ruins: both were built by somebody who could measure the strength and could not measure the absence, so the same experiment was run twice and returned nothing twice.
