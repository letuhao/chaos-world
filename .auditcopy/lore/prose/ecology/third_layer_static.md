# Third Layer Static

The third layer's ecology, which is the absence of one: nothing on it grows, recovers, adapts or is cheap, and the only thing that happens there is law being written nine more times.

It exists because at fifteen slots over three layers there is more law present than one will can hold, and a system with a permanent surplus has no room for anything that needs to accumulate - including life.

That makes the top tier's ecology a ranking rather than a web. Every organism and every person on the third layer is placed by how much surplus it can stand in, and nothing there has a population that recovers once it drops, because recovery needs headroom and there is none.
