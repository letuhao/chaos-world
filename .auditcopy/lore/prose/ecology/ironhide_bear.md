# Ironhide Bear

The plains' great grazer, whose hide is the only armour the mortal tier can make and whose range follows worked ore.

It exists because life law is written in the mortal tier and qi law is spent at the floor of its range - which is exactly enough for an animal to reach combat power 5 to 50 and to be hunted for a hide that stops a blade.

It is also the tier's ecological anchor: it grazes the herb belt that is the only medicine on the plains, so the population that protects the crop is the population that eats it, and every hunt on the Bitter Scrub is a decision about medicine as much as about meat.
