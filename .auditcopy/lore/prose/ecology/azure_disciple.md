# Azure Disciple

The plains' humanoid visitor: a body authored only from the spirit tier up, which makes every one of them on the Mortal Plains an immigrant from a world with a larger law budget.

It exists because a mortal-tier world licenses plant and beast life only, so a humanoid there is not a resident population - it is a person standing on ground that never expected one, and every dispute about who owns the Cairn Field is a dispute about what to do with the people using it.

That makes this species the setting's entire argument about borders in one body: they can be refused at the stair, they can be taxed at the ring, they cannot be made to disappear, and nobody on the plains has the law to make them leave.
