# The Overlayer Drift

The second layer's ecology: the slow upward movement of surplus law off the Immortal Court's surface, which is what the Overlayer Shelf is made of and what feeds the Ember Ley from below.

It exists because ten slots over six groups produces four duplicate imprints and the surface cannot carry them, so the excess has to go somewhere; it goes up, and once it is up it behaves like weather - it pools, drifts and settles.

Which means the ley below and the shelf above are one system rather than two features: the ley burns what the drift delivers, the shelf is where the drift has finished pooling, and a party controlling one is negotiating with a supply line it does not own.
