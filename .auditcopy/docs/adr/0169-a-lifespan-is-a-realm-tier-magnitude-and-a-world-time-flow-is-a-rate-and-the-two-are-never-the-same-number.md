# 0169 A lifespan is a realm-tier MAGNITUDE and a world time-flow is a RATE, and the two are never the same number

- Status: Proposed
- Date: 2026-10-04
- Amends: ADR 0109 (the lifespan gap it records as owned), AGENTS.md 139-146 (the magnitude/rate split)
- Depends on: ADR 0050 (magnitudes are authored data), 0066 / 0116 (the one rate), 0167 (the period is the time unit)

## Context

- **Nothing reads `race_lifespan`.** `RaceStats.LIFESPAN` (`modules/race/stats.gd:20`) is written once by `RaceProvider` (`:33`) and consumed only as a dict echo (`api.gd:133,158,197`) or by one test asserting it equals the authored default (`tests/modules/race/test_race_attach.gd:216`).
- **There is no age at all.** grep for `age_years` / `elapsed_days` / `born_year` over `src/` returns zero; `Actor.to_dict()` (`core/actor.gd:325-350`) serializes no age. The only `age` in the tree is `SocialBond.age` (`modules/social/social_bond.gd:70`), a bond-strengthening counter.
- **Four tiers, no duration.** The ladder is 9 Mortal / 9 Spirit / 9 Immortal / 3 Transcendent (`core/realm_defaults.gd:4,7-10`) and `RealmDef.tier` is already an authored `1..4`. Authored races: emberblood 18,250 d, commonborn 36,500, stoneborn 54,000, tidecaller 58,400 (`data/races/*.tres`; default `race_def.gd:56` = 36,500). Every one is a ~50-160 year **mortal** body, so a Spirit cultivator and a mortal share one ceiling and every long meditation is arbitrary. `test_race_content.gd:174` already concedes it: *"`lifespan` is data nothing enforces yet - ADR 0109 records that gap."*
- **A ~100x time-rate spread exists with no engine.** `time_flow_min/max` (`world_tier_def.gd:14-15`) are authored 0.5-2.0 / 1.0-10.0 / 5.0-50.0 / 10.0-100.0 across four `.tres`, and the only `load()` of them anywhere is `tests/modules/world/test_world_defs.gd:28,47,65,83`; `modules/world/api.gd` has no tier loader. Also dead: `InsideWorld.time_flow` (`core/inside_world.gd:17`, published as `&"inside_world_time_flow"` at `core/inside_world_provider.gd:16`, zero production readers), `WorldState.time_flow_rate` (`core/world_creation.gd:36`, serialize-only), and all six `data/world/laws/*.tres` — including `temporal_law.tres`, whose own description reads "Governs time flow rate and temporal stability within the world."

## Decision

- **A lifespan is a MAGNITUDE and a world time-flow is a RATE. They are different kinds of number and may never be the same value, derived from each other, or combined.** A magnitude answers *how strong/long is a thing from this realm*; a rate answers *what is one unit of this realm's time worth*. They already collide on paper: the Mortal lifespan multiplier is `1.0` and `spirit_world.time_flow_min` is `1.0`, and they mean opposite things. Combining them is dimensionally void — days x (days per day) is not days — and that conversion belongs to the clock owning both, never to either table.
- **`RaceDef.lifespan` stays exactly as authored: the MORTAL-TIER BASELINE of a body.** No race `.tres` changes. An actor's lifespan is `def.lifespan x multiplier_for(realm.tier)`, and **that multiplication happens once, in `core`** — no module authors, reads, or re-does it.
- **The multiplier table is authored data in ONE place, keyed by `RealmDef.tier`.** `core/realm_lifespan_table.tres` (+ `realm_lifespan_table.gd`, `class_name RealmLifespan`), preloaded by `RealmDefaults` beside `POWER`, exactly as `core/realm_power_table.tres` is. Four rows, hand-editable, a decade ladder:

  | tier | band | multiplier | emberblood | commonborn | stoneborn | tidecaller |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Mortal | R1-9 | **1.0** | 50 y | 100 y | 148 y | 160 y |
  | Spirit | R10-18 | **10.0** | 500 y | 1,000 y | 1,479 y | 1,600 y |
  | Immortal | R19-27 | **100.0** | 5,000 y | 10,000 y | 14,795 y | 16,000 y |
  | Transcendent | R28-30 | **1,000.0** | 50,000 y | 100,000 y | 147,945 y | 160,000 y |

  (365 d/y is a *reading convention for this table only* — the calendar and persistence schema are not decided here.) Keyed by the authored `tier`, never by `RealmDef.index`: a realm inserted inside a band inherits that band's number, which is the correct answer. A missing row resolves to **1.0, never 0.0**, the same fallback as `realm_power_table.gd:32`; a fifth tier is a new authored row, not a formula.
- **The numbers are authored, never computed.** "x10 per tier" is the *shape the author chose*, not a recipe: a tier is not a ladder position, and a curve from the index is what ADR 0042/0050 deleted.
- **A retreat must fit inside a life at the tier that offers it, and the headroom is scale-invariant.** Retreats ride the same decade ladder as the lifespan — 10 y / 100 y / 1,000 y / 10,000 y — so **every** tier gets the same 5.0x headroom against the *shortest* authored race and 16.0x against the longest: `1,000 y / 5,000 y = 0.20` (Immortal, emberblood) and `10,000 / 50,000 = 0.20` (Transcendent, emberblood); `1,000 / 16,000 = 0.0625` and `10,000 / 160,000 = 0.0625` at tidecaller. A century-scale meditation is affordable at Spirit and catastrophic at Mortal — the fiction, expressed — and no tier's retreat ever silently becomes cheap against a life. **The ratio is what carries the claim, not a period count**: the two ladders are both decades, so the ratio is scale-invariant whatever ratio a period carries to a year. That decoupling is deliberate — the period count was removed when ADR 0167 ceded the calendar to the clock SSOT (ADR 0173), and the headroom argument is stronger for it.
- **`race_lifespan` publishes the EFFECTIVE lifespan** at the actor's current highest realm (`RealmScaling.highest_realm`), so the one authored baseline and the one tier row meet in a single stat. The raw value stays readable off `RaceDef`; no second derived stat is added.
- **This ADR does NOT wire world time-flow, and says so deliberately.** The multiplier is keyed by **realm tier** (a `core/` property of the actor); time-flow is keyed by **world tier** (a `world`-module vocabulary: `mortal_world`/`spirit_world`/`immortal_world`/`transcendent_world`). The two band sets coincide at 1-9 / 10-18 / 19-27 / 28-30 — but only in dead data: `realm_min`/`realm_max` (`world_tier_def.gd:8-9`) is read by no production code, and `modules/world/api.gd` has no tier loader to read it with. Two vocabularies that agree by coincidence are what gets merged by accident. Lifespan therefore reads `RealmDef.tier` and **never** a `world` field, a `WorldState` or an `InsideWorld`. Time-flow stays authored-and-dead until the clock ADR: it needs a tier loader, a consumer for `time_flow`, and an owner for `WorldState.time_flow_rate` — none of which this ADR would survive being mixed into. Decided here is only the **negative**: no lifespan code path may read it.
- **These are MAGNITUDES. The multiplier must never be computed from, or share a curve with, `RealmRate.RATE_STEP`** (`core/realm_rate.gd:76`) — the exact failure `AGENTS.md:141` records: *"reading a shared exponential as a gain is what once made a single breakthrough worth more than everything else combined"* (ADR 0066). `RATE_STEP^29 = 1.776` is a gain; this table spans 1000x. They may not share a constant, a recipe, or a fixture.
- **The guard reads SOURCE, because `tools arch` cannot see this.** `BARE_REF_UNITS` excludes `modules/*`, so `tests/core/test_realm_lifespan_table.gd` mirrors `tests/core/test_realm_rate.gd:208-224` and fails if any file under `src/` outside the table declares a lifespan multiplier (`LIFESPAN_SCALE`, `lifespan_multiplier`, a tier-keyed dict), naming file and line. Value assertions alone are insufficient and ADR 0116 has the mutation: a numerically-identical private copy stayed green under every value check (**403 passed / 3 failed**, only the structural pins firing). The same test pins shape (four rows and no others, Mortal at exactly 1.0, each row >= 10x the row below, finite) and the two invariances — change a `RealmDef.power` or a `RATE_STEP`, `RealmLifespan` is unchanged.
- **What crossing the lifespan DOES is not decided here; the number is built to be sufficient for it.** No age field, no death-by-age path, and `app/` holds no ledger — death runs through `app/soul_death.gd` and `modules/soul`. Committed: the number is one **absolute duration in days**, comparable against an elapsed age and an authored retreat cost, keyed by tier so a future soft cap (a breakthrough gated by remaining life) reads it without a second scale. Death or cap is a real decision, owed its own ADR.
- **This is the ADR for the `core/` change** — a `core/` addition requires one, and the table is the substance of it.

## Consequences

- A Transcendent cultivator can sit in a retreat for a millennium and a mortal cannot survive a decade-long one, off four authored cells. `emberblood` stays the shortest body at every tier, so the fiction survives the multiplier.
- One place to retune, no formula to re-derive, no recipe to guard against. `realm_lifespan check` may assert shape only.
- **`data/world/tiers/*.tres` and `InsideWorld.time_flow` stay dead and stay separately owned.** Two ~100x ladders now exist in the repo — one live, one not — and this ADR is the line between them, not a bridge.
- **Open, deferred:** death-by-age vs soft cap and the soul/rebirth interaction; the calendar and persistence schema for an age field (`Actor.to_dict()` today serializes none); the `world` tier loader and what consumes `time_flow_min/max`, including whether `WorldState.time_flow_rate` and `InsideWorld.time_flow` collapse to one owner; and whether `realm_min`/`realm_max` ever becomes real, which would make the two tier vocabularies visibly one fact in two files.
