# Art Criteria Index — Ilsa Renn (unique-0001)

> **Art style**: `cultivation-fantasy-relic` — desaturated, ink contours heavier than fill, line is the drawing and wash is the correction. Unusually desaturated for a cultivation palette because the character reads as the baseline against which colour is measured.

---

## 1. Character Art Direction Summary

### What Makes Her Visually Unique

Ilsa Renn is not "plain" as a default — she is plain as a **design achievement**. Every visual choice is a deliberate subtraction. In a world of glowing qi, clan marks, and cultivation scarring, she is the one body that reads as **nothing at all**. The art direction must make the viewer feel the *absence* of signal, not just a lack of decoration.

**The core visual paradox**: she must be visually forgettable yet narratively unforgettable. The art should make the viewer feel that they've already forgotten what she looks like, even while looking at her.

**Specific visual identity markers:**

| Feature | What to render | What to suppress |
|---|---|---|
| **Face** | Ordinary, unremarkable, no striking features. Slightly tired. The kind of face you can't reconstruct from memory. | Beauty, symmetry, large eyes, delicate features, any "anime" facial structure |
| **Hair** | Dark brown, cropped short, **cut with a blade** — uneven ends, practical, no style. Reads as "nothing" to a gate. | Gloss, shine, flowing locks, styled bangs, hair ornaments |
| **Eyes** | Dark, level, very steady. No glow, no light reflection, no readable intent. The unsettling part is how *little* they report. | Sparkle, highlights, visible emotion, "piercing" gaze |
| **Skin** | Ordinary warm human skin. No flush, no scar tissue, no qi-sheen. Slightly weathered at 41. | Perfection, glow, translucency, any supernatural quality |
| **Build** | Average, unadapted. The frame of someone who has never been asked to carry a load. Not thin, not muscular — *unremarkable*. | Athletic build, exaggerated proportions, any heroic silhouette |
| **Attire** | Undyed wool travelling coat over high-collared linen shift. Sleeves full, wrists bound with plain cord. No livery, no sect colour, no rank marking. Boots resoled so often they no longer read as a pattern. | Armour, decorative trim, bright colours, visible fastenings, any identifying marks |
| **Marks** | **None.** No clan mark, no oath burn, no cultivation scarring. Unmarked forearms. The absence is the single most alarming thing about her. | Any tattoo, scar, brand, or marking whatsoever |
| **Bearing** | Absolutely still by default. Trained stillness — stands where she was left standing, does not fidget, shift, or fill a silence. | Dynamic posing, contrapposto, any sense of performance |

**Palette rule**: She is deliberately the least colourful person in any room. If a shot has other characters or elements, they must carry more colour than she does.

### Silhouette Keywords

- **Forgettable** — the silhouette should not be memorable
- **Upright** — spine straight, no slouch, no swagger
- **Contained** — arms close to body, no expansive gestures
- **Still** — the silhouette reads as a person waiting, not acting
- **Unadorned** — no protrusions, no weapons, no accessories beyond the coat and cord
- **Ordinary** — the silhouette of a person you would pass without noticing

### Personality Through Posture

Ilsa's body language is **trained stillness**. She has spent 40 years being the world's baseline, and it shows in a posture that is:

- **Perfectly vertical** — no lean, no weight shift, no casual asymmetry
- **Hands open and visible** — as though being read, always
- **Still when waiting** — she does not pace, does not fidget, does not fill silence
- **Sits before agreeing to sit** — standing is never on the record as consent
- **Rests one hand on a column, sill, or doorframe** when waiting — the only permitted relaxation
- **Counts under her breath** when surprised — the only visible crack in the stillness

## 3. Diversity Rules

### No Two Shots Should Feel the Same

- **Vary the camera angle**: eye level, low angle, high angle, profile, rear, three-quarter — no two shots share the same angle.
- **Vary the distance**: close-up, waist-up, full figure, wide environmental — no two shots share the same framing.
- **Vary the light**: even, warm, hard side, soft low, upper-left — no two shots share the same lighting.
- **Vary the context**: plain field, doorway, measurement yard, gate corridor, graveside, hall — no two shots share the same environment.
- **Vary the activity**: waiting, correcting, reading, walking, sitting, standing — no two shots share the same action.

### Each Expression Must Be Dramatically Different

Do not vary expressions by mouth shape alone. Vary the **entire face and body**:

| Expression | Eye direction | Head position | Body language | Light |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Neutral | Straight out | Level | Relaxed | Even |

## 4. Prompt Engineering Guidelines

### How to Suppress the Model's Attractiveness Bias

The model will default to "beautiful anime girl" unless explicitly suppressed. Use these techniques:

1. **Lead with "plain" and "unremarkable"** in the prompt — these are the strongest suppressors.
2. **Specify "ordinary warm human skin, no flush, no scar tissue, no qi-sheen"** — the absence of supernatural beauty markers.
3. **Specify "average build, unadapted"** — suppresses the athletic/heroic default.
4. **Specify "dark brown hair, cropped short, cut with a blade, uneven ends"** — suppresses the long, glossy, styled default.
5. **Specify "dark, level, steady eyes, no glow, no light reflection"** — suppresses the sparkling anime eye default.
6. **Use "the kind of face you can't reconstruct from memory"** — a conceptual instruction that works better than a list of negatives.
7. **Include "deliberately the least colourful person in any room"** — forces desaturation.

### How to Force "Plain and Unremarkable"

1. **Use the word "unremarkable" repeatedly** — it is the single most effective suppressor.
2. **Specify "no striking features"** — the model will not add any.
3. **Specify "the absence of signal"** — frames the plainness as intentional.
4. **Use "forgettable" as a positive instruction** — "a silhouette that is not memorable."
5. **Specify "no clan mark, no oath burn, no cultivation scarring"** — removes the default fantasy character markers.
6. **Use "the face of a person who is not hiding anything"** — implies no secrets, no depth, no mystery.
7. **Specify "an expression that would read identically on anyone standing here"** — forces genericness.

### How to Make Expressions Dramatic and Distinct

Do not rely on mouth shape. Vary the **entire face**:

1. **Eye direction**: straight out, down, away, up, wide, set, unfocused — each expression has a different gaze.
2. **Head position**: level, lowered, tipped back, turned, chin down, square — each expression has a different head angle.
3. **Body language**: relaxed, leaning in, shoulder lifted, shoulders down, hands together, arm raised, weight forward, still, holding — each expression has a different body.
4. **Light**: even, warm, hard side, soft low, upper left — each expression has a different light quality.
5. **Use conceptual descriptions**: "a small hard courtesy that does not reach the eyes" is more effective than "slight smile."
6. **Use absence**: "grief held entirely motionless" is more dramatic than "crying."
7. **Use contradiction**: "weariness threaded with defiance" is more interesting than "tired."

### How to Make Poses Dynamic with Varied Angles

1. **Specify the camera angle explicitly**: "low three-quarter," "profile," "three-quarter rear," "low angle," "eye level."
2. **Specify the framing explicitly**: "full figure," "waist-up," "close head and shoulders," "wide environmental."
3. **Specify the ground plane**: "ground plane visible," "platform edge visible," "grave mound in frame."
4. **Use architectural elements**: "doorway centred," "column vertical through the frame," "canopy overhead."
5. **Use motion blur selectively**: "crowd as blurred motion only" — keeps her sharp while the world moves.
6. **Use focus selectively**: "listeners out of focus" — keeps her sharp while others blur.
7. **Vary the height**: standing, seated on platform, seated on ground, walking — no two poses share the same height.
