# Asian Hell World Pack: Địa Ngục Giới (Minh Giới / Yin-Jian)
*Based on Classical East Asian Underworld Mythology & the Grand Cosmic World-Building of "Vạn Cổ Thần Đế" (Eternal God Emperor)*

Comprehensive 600+ world map asset collection for the Asian Netherworld Realm (**Địa Ngục Giới / 冥界**), designed for Chaos World's Action RPG combat, adventure exploration, puzzle mechanics, and world simulator / domain management.

* **Total Assets**: 629
* **Standard Tile Unit**: 128x128 px
* **Sub-cell Collision Unit**: 32x32 px (4x4 subcells per tile)
* **Art Direction**: Top-down orthographic (~45°), hand-painted gouache, fine ink contours (`#263A35`), upper-left directional lighting (315°), rich East Asian / Sino-Vietnamese underworld palette.

## 1. Cosmological Structure & Sovereign Nations (Địa Ngục Thập Tộc & Các Đại Thế Lực)

Unlike traditional folklore which views Hell solely as a subterranean punitive prison (the 18 courts of Diyu), this pack models Hell as a boundless **Cosmic Civilization and Multiverse of Sovereign Nations**, mirroring the grand lore of *Vạn Cổ Thần Đế*:

### A. The Supreme Imperial Clan (Địa Ngục Đệ Nhất Tộc)
* **Diêm La Tộc (Yanluo Clan / 阎罗族)**: The undisputed highest sovereign clan. Alive (not undead), possessing supreme royal bloodlines, coiling dragon mounts, golden-black obsidian celestial palaces, and sacred Yanluo Divine Trees (*Diêm La Thần Thụ*). Governs the cosmic duality of Life and Death.

### B. Thượng Tam Tộc (Upper Three Spiritual Clans)
* **Minh Tộc (Nether Clan / 冥族)**: Born of pure primordial Nether Qi and Death Qi. Masters of ancient curses, hex steles, and soul-binding formations (*Minh Tộc Chú Văn Thần Miếu*).
* **Quỷ Tộc (Ghost Clan / 鬼族)**: Ethereal soul cultivators inhabiting boundless floating spectral metropolises (*Huyền Không Quỷ Thành*), cyan soul lanterns, and soulstone currency networks.
* **Thi Tộc (Corpse Clan / 尸族)**: Ancient reanimated sovereigns and alchemists operating colossal Corpse Nurturing Pools (*Dưỡng Thi Trì*), toxic corpse chimneys, and bronze sarcophagus vaults.

### C. Trung Tam Tộc (Middle Three Carnal & Warfare Clans)
* **Bất Tử Huyết Tộc (Immortal Blood Clan / 不死血族)**: Aristocratic winged blood predators divided into 10 great tribes. Features gothic bat-wing citadels (*Dực Lâu Cổ Bảo*), sacred blood holy springs (*Huyết Thánh Tuyền*), blood grape vineyards, and blood altars.
* **La Sát Tộc (Rakshasa Clan / 罗刹族)**: Extreme cultural and biological dimorphism:
  * *Feminine Ruling Class*: Exquisite, enchanting diplomat-queens dwelling in floating pale jade and purple silk pavilions (*Ngọc Ty Lâu*), wielding illusion arts.
  * *Masculine Legions*: Gargantuan horned war juggernauts in spiked iron bastions (*Chiến Lũy*) commanding six-horned war rhinos.
* **Tu La Tộc (Asura Clan / 修罗族)**: Dedicated entirely to martial perfection and Slaughter Intent (*Sát Ý*). Blade duel arenas (*Quyết Đấu Lôi Đài*), spiraling battle towers, and blood-quenched armories.

### D. Hạ Tam Tộc (Lower Three Inorganic & Void Clans)
* **Cốt Tộc (Bone Clan / 骨族)**: Sentient skeletal entities powered by violet soul flames. Entire cities carved from titan ribcages and ossuary mountains, equipped with soul-flame bone kilns.
* **Thạch Tộc (Stone Clan / 石族)**: Living basalt colossi, obsidian titans, and ancient petrified forests linked to tectonic earth veins and gravity-shifting dials.
* **Hắc Ám Tộc (Dark Clan / 黑暗族)**: Void entities wielding light-devouring obelisks (*Thôn Quang Ma Châm*), shadow temples, and invisible stalkers.

### E. Transcendent Powers, Cosmopolitan Hubs & Frontlines
* **Vận Mệnh Thần Điện (Fane of Destiny / 命运神殿)**: The cosmic spiritual summit atop Destiny Mountain. Features the Gate of Destiny (*Vận Mệnh Chi Môn*), the Cosmic Loom of Fate, and the Twelve Divisional Sub-Fanes (Death, Calamity, Auspiciousness, Heavenly Punishment).
* **Bách Tộc Vương Thành (City of a Hundred Royal Races / 百族王城)**: The colossal multi-cultural trade capital of 100+ minor underworld clans. Features multi-racial bazaars, mercenary guilds, and underground black auctions.
* **Tam Đồ Hà (San Tu River / 三途河)**: The primordial galactic river ferrying souls and corpses. Ancient corpse barges, nether ghost reeds (*Minh Vi*), and mysterious ancient ferrymen.
* **Chiến Trường Công Đức & Thần Lạc Vực (Battlefield of Merits & God-Fall Domain / 功德战场 & 神落之地)**: The cosmic frontline against the Celestial Court. Fossilized god rib ridges, boiling divine blood craters, titanic shattered god halberds, and forward command bastions.
* **Traditional Diyu (Thập Điện Diêm La & Phong Đô)**: The classical foundational layer with the Ten Yama Courts, Meng Po teahouses, Naihe Bridge, Karma Mirrors, and copper purgatory pillars.

## 4. Integration into Chaos World Gameplay

* **Domain & World Simulator**: Players can interact diplomatically, wage conquest wars, or establish trade pacts between the 10 Hell Clans and the City of a Hundred Races.
* **Race-Specific Industry & Cultivation**: Build Blood Holy Springs to refine blood pills, operate Corpse Nurturing Pools to produce armored vanguards, harvest Yanluo Divine Trees for breakthrough Dao fruits, or smelt bone armaments in Bone Kilns.
* **Dynamic Fog-of-War & Light Mechanics**: Dark Clan Light-Devouring Obelisks dynamically absorb player sight radii, requiring Yin-Yang mirrors or Ghost Lanterns to dispel.
* **High-Stakes Puzzles & World Events**: Manipulating the Loom of Fate or meditating before the Slaughter Intent Stele directly impacts realm drop tables, monster spawn aggression, and karmic tribulations.
