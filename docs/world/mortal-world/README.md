# Mortal World (凡界)

## Nature

- The **baseline plane** — where all cultivation begins
- Physical laws dominate; qi is thin and scattered
- **World seeds** germinate here — cosmic eggs that spawn new realms
- **Karma accumulates** here — every action has cosmic weight

## Inhabitants

- **Humans** — mortal cultivators, sects, kingdoms
- **Beasts** — wild animals with latent spiritual potential
- **Plants** — herbs and trees with medicinal properties
- **Ghosts** — spirits of the unresolved dead

## Factions

| Faction | Dao Alignment | Nature |
|---|---|---|
| **Great Sects** | Mixed | Hereditary powers controlling resources |
| **Kingdoms** | Body Dao | Mortal empires with martial traditions |
| **Trade Guilds** | Qi Dao | Merchant alliances controlling spirit stone flow |
| **Underworld** | Mind Dao | Ghost administration, death management |

## Resources

- **Spirit stones** — condensed qi; the universal currency
- **Spirit herbs** — medicinal plants for pill refinement
- **World seeds** — rare primordial artifacts (ultimate strategic resource)
- **Ancient ruins** — remnants of fallen civilizations with lost techniques

## Conflicts

- **Sect wars** — battles for spirit vein ownership
- **Kingdom conquests** — territorial expansion for resources
- **Beast tides** — periodic monster migrations threatening settlements
- **Resource depletion** — over-mining forces expansion into others' territory

## World Mechanics

- **Karma system** — good/evil actions accumulate cosmic debt
- **Reincarnation** — souls cycle through six paths based on karma
- **Heavenly Tribulation** — lightning gates breakthroughs to Spirit World
- **World seed germination** — seeds absorb qi and grow into new realms
