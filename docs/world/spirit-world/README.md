# Spirit World (灵界)

## Nature

- The **plane of manifested laws** — qi is dense and visible
- **World laws** become tangible — elemental affinities shape reality
- **Six Paths of Reincarnation** operate here — souls are sorted by karma
- Time flows differently — 1 year here = 10 years in Mortal World

## Inhabitants

- **Spirit cultivators** — beings who ascended from Mortal World
- **Nature spirits** — elementals, plant spirits, beast spirits
- **Ghosts** — unresolved spirits seeking resolution or revenge
- **Asuras** — warlike demi-gods driven by ambition and jealousy

## Factions

| Faction | Dao Alignment | Nature |
|---|---|---|
| **Elemental Courts** | Qi Dao | Alliances of nature spirits controlling elemental domains |
| **Asura Clans** | Body Dao | Warrior demi-gods seeking to overthrow the heavens |
| **Ghost Kingdoms** | Mind Dao | Spirit administrations managing the dead |
| **Independent Sects** | Mixed | Cultivators who reject heavenly bureaucracy |

## Resources

- **Spirit veins** — concentrated qi flows (depletable)
- **Elemental cores** — crystallized elemental energy
- **Karmic crystals** — solidified karma; can be traded or consumed
- **Ancient battlefields** — sites where the Dao is thin; easier comprehension

## Conflicts

- **Asura-Deva war** — eternal struggle between chaos-gods and order-gods
- **Elemental territorial disputes** — spirit courts fight over elemental domains
- **Ghost rebellions** — unresolved spirits resist reincarnation sorting
- **Resource wars** — spirit vein depletion drives inter-faction conflict

## World Mechanics

- **Six Paths sorting** — souls are judged and sent to appropriate realms
- **Elemental manifestation** — qi takes physical form (fire, water, etc.)
- **Karmic visibility** — karma becomes a tangible, tradeable resource
- **Tribulation escalation** — breakthroughs face stronger heavenly tribulations
