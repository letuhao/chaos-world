# Immortal World (仙界)

## Nature

- The **plane of heavenly order** — the Heavenly Court governs here
- **World evolution** is visible — worlds grow or die based on cultivator activity
- **Heavenly bureaucracy** — even gods have ranks and duties
- **World laws** are fully customizable by immortals

## Inhabitants

- **Immortals** — beings who transcended mortality
- **Heavenly officials** — bureaucratic gods administering cosmic law
- **Ancient sects** — hereditary powers with millennia of history
- **Demon clan** — chaotic opposition to heavenly order

## Factions

| Faction | Dao Alignment | Nature |
|---|---|---|
| **Heavenly Court** | Mind Dao | Cosmic bureaucracy enforcing order |
| **Ancient Sects** | Mixed | Hereditary powers with deep resources |
| **Demon Clan** | Body Dao | Chaotic warriors seeking to overthrow order |
| **Buddhist Order** | Mind Dao | Enlightenment-focused; transcends conflict |

## Resources

- **Immortal herbs** — plants that grow only in immortal realms
- **World seeds** — can be cultivated into full worlds
- **Dao comprehension sites** — locations where the Dao is accessible
- **Ancient inheritances** — legacies of extinct immortal civilizations

## Conflicts

- **Heavenly Court vs. Demon Clan** — order vs. chaos (cyclical, never resolved)
- **Sect wars** — ancient powers fight over dao comprehension sites
- **World evolution competition** — worlds compete to evolve and absorb others
- **Ancient awakening** — sealed primordial beings wake and reclaim territory

## World Mechanics

- **Heavenly Tribulation** — the Court "prunes" worlds that grow too powerful
- **World evolution** — worlds can grow, merge, or die based on cultivator activity
- **Proxy wars** — top-tier beings fight through mortal champions (direct conflict = mutual destruction)
- **Ascension gates** — breakthroughs to Transcendent tier require stable worlds
