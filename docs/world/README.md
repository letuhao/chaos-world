# World Overview

## Four World Tiers

| Tier | Name | Realms | Inhabitants | Key Feature |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Mortal World** (凡界) | 1–9 | Humans, beasts, plants | World seeds germinate; karma accumulates |
| 2 | **Spirit World** (灵界) | 10–18 | Spirit cultivators, ghosts, nature spirits | World laws manifest; six paths operate |
| 3 | **Immortal World** (仙界) | 19–27 | Immortals, sects, heavenly officials | Heavenly Court governs; world evolution visible |
| 4 | **Transcendent World** (道界) | 28–30 | Dao Ancestors, Primordial beings | World creation/destruction; cosmic conflicts |

## Three Major Dao Factions

| Dao | Philosophy | Color | Core Belief |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Qi Dao** (气道) | Way of Energy | Azure | Cultivate Qi to harmonize with the universe |
| **Body Dao** (体道) | Way of Form | Gold | Cultivate the body to become an indestructible vessel |
| **Mind Dao** (神道) | Way of Spirit | Violet | Cultivate the mind to transcend physical limitations |

## World Relationships

- Each tier is a **plane** — a state of existence, not a physical location
- **Ascension** = dimensional promotion, not physical travel
- Higher tiers can observe/intervene in lower tiers (with restrictions)
- **World seeds** spawn in Mortal World, mature through tiers
- Destroyed worlds **collapse downward** (Immortal → Spirit → Mortal)

## Conflict Drivers

1. **Resource scarcity** — world seeds, spirit stones, immortal herbs are finite
2. **Ideological divide** — Order vs. Chaos vs. Freedom
3. **Karmic debt** — worlds accumulate karma; excess triggers tribulation
4. **World evolution** — worlds compete to evolve; absorbing weaker worlds accelerates growth
5. **Ancient awakening** — sleeping ancients wake and reclaim former territories

## Alliance Patterns

- Temporary alliances against common threats
- Marriage alliances between sects/clans for bloodline fusion
- Trade alliances for resource exchange
- Dao partnerships for dual cultivation
- Heavenly Court treaties — bureaucracy mediates between factions

## Betrayal Patterns

- **Resource betrayal** — allies turn when a world seed appears
- **Dao betrayal** — a cultivator's Dao shifts, making former allies enemies
- **Reincarnation betrayal** — past-life enemies become present enemies
- **Heavenly Court manipulation** — bureaucracy plays factions against each other
- **Karmic betrayal** — accumulated karma forces a faction to act against its nature

## Cosmic Cycle

- Worlds are born, live, die, and are reborn in **endless cycles**
- Each cycle produces a slightly different world — not pure repetition
- **Dead realm** — world without qi; becomes wasteland or graveyard
- **Ascended realm** — world transformed into a higher-plane entity
- **Sealed realm** — world frozen in time as tomb, prison, or time capsule
- **Reborn realm** — destroyed world's essence survives to create a new world
