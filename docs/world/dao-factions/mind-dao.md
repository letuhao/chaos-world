# Mind Dao (神道) — The Way of Spirit

## Philosophy

- **Core belief**: Cultivate the mind/spirit to transcend physical limitations
- **Inspired by**: Buddhist Formless Realm, Christian Holy Spirit, Shinto Kami
- **Cosmic function**: **Transcendence** — liberation from form

## Practice

- Meditation (consciousness expansion)
- Soul cultivation (spirit strengthening)
- Illusion (sensory manipulation, mental attacks)
- Precognition (fate reading, probability manipulation)

## Strengths

- Illusion and mental attacks
- Precognition and fate reading
- Possession and spiritual domination
- Transcends physical limitations

## Weaknesses

- Physically fragile
- Vulnerable to Body Dao (form overpowers formless)
- Requires extreme mental discipline
- Risk of spiritual deviation (madness)

## Faction Color

**Violet / Purple**

## World Alignment

- **Immortal World** — Heavenly Court, Buddhist Order
- **Transcendent World** — Dao comprehension, primordial truth

## Conflict with Other Dao

| Opponent | Nature | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| Qi Dao | Spirit vs. Energy | Harmony through meditation |
| Body Dao | Formless vs. Form | Enlightenment through physical discipline |
