# Body Dao (体道) — The Way of Form

## Philosophy

- **Core belief**: Cultivate the body to become an indestructible vessel
- **Inspired by**: Buddhist Form Realm, Greek Olympians, Egyptian physical preservation
- **Cosmic function**: **Preservation** — endurance through time

## Practice

- Body refinement (tempering flesh, bone, organ)
- Martial arts (combat techniques, movement)
- Physical transformation (size, shape, density)
- Weapon fusion (body as weapon)

## Strengths

- Resilient and durable
- Close combat supremacy
- Immune to most energy attacks
- Self-sufficient (no external qi needed)

## Weaknesses

- Slow progression compared to Qi Dao
- Limited ranged capability
- Vulnerable to Mind Dao illusions
- Requires rare materials for body refinement

## Faction Color

**Gold / Yellow**

## World Alignment

- **Mortal World** — kingdoms, martial traditions
- **Immortal World** — demon clan, warrior sects

## Conflict with Other Dao

| Opponent | Nature | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| Qi Dao | Matter vs. Energy | Balance through dual cultivation |
| Mind Dao | Form vs. Formless | Enlightenment through physical discipline |
