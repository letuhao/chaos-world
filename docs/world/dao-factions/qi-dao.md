# Qi Dao (气道) — The Way of Energy

## Philosophy

- **Core belief**: Cultivate Qi (energy) to harmonize with the universe
- **Inspired by**: Daoist Three Pure Ones (Lingbao — Numinous Treasure), Hindu Brahma (Creator)
- **Cosmic function**: **Creation** — bringing forth from nothing

## Practice

- Energy circulation through meridians
- Elemental manipulation (fire, water, wood, metal, earth)
- Internal alchemy (pill refinement, qi condensation)
- Technique diversity — hundreds of qi-based techniques

## Strengths

- Versatile and adaptable to any situation
- Powerful ranged techniques
- Fast cultivation when qi is abundant
- Can harmonize with other dao through dual cultivation

## Weaknesses

- Dependent on external qi sources
- Vulnerable to qi deviation (走火入魔)
- Weak in low-qi environments
- Body is fragile compared to Body Dao

## Faction Color

**Azure / Blue**

## World Alignment

- **Spirit World** — elemental courts, qi-rich environments
- **Transcendent World** — primordial qi manipulation

## Conflict with Other Dao

| Opponent | Nature | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| Body Dao | Energy vs. Matter | Balance through dual cultivation |
| Mind Dao | Energy vs. Spirit | Harmony through meditation |
