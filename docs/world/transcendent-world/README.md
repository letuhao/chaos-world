# Transcendent World (道界)

## Nature

- The **plane of primordial truth** — where the Dao itself is accessible
- **World creation/destruction** is routine — beings shape reality
- **Cosmic conflicts** — the final battleground for ideological supremacy
- **Time is non-linear** — past, present, and future coexist

## Inhabitants

- **Dao Ancestors** — beings who have comprehended the Dao
- **Primordial beings** — entities that existed before the current cosmic order
- **Heavenly Emperor** — the supreme ruler (if one exists)
- **Chaos entities** — beings of pure entropy seeking to unmake reality

## Factions

| Faction | Dao Alignment | Nature |
|---|---|---|
| **Dao Alliance** | Mixed | United front of Dao Ancestors |
| **Primordial Court** | Body Dao | Ancient beings seeking to restore the old order |
| **Chaos Legion** | Mind Dao | Entities of entropy seeking cosmic dissolution |
| **Heavenly Emperor** | Qi Dao | Supreme authority (if one exists) |

## Resources

- **Primordial qi** — the raw energy of creation
- **World essences** — the fundamental building blocks of reality
- **Dao fragments** — pieces of the Dao itself; comprehension = power
- **Chaos seeds** — entropy made tangible; can corrupt or destroy worlds

## Conflicts

- **Primordial vs. Current Order** — old beings vs. new cosmic order
- **Dao comprehension races** — factions race to comprehend the Dao first
- **World creation wars** — battles over the right to create/destroy worlds
- **Cosmic cycle** — the universe undergoes periodic destruction/recreation

## World Mechanics

- **World creation** — beings can create worlds from primordial qi
- **World destruction** — worlds can be unmade by chaos entities
- **Dao comprehension** — the true power source; comprehension = reality manipulation
- **Cosmic cycle** — the universe dies and is reborn; each cycle is slightly different
